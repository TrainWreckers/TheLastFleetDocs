Parameters

AutoReach float

How near (across, on its own floor) anyone comes for a sliding door to open, meters.

CloseSeconds float

How long a sliding door stays open once nobody is near it, seconds.

ClearReach float

How near its middle anyone keeps a door from shutting, meters: nobody's shut in a doorway.

HandReach float

How near a player's eye a door opened by hand is reached, meters.

SoldierReach float

How near a door opened by hand a soldier comes to open it, meters.

SoldierSeconds float

How long a soldier takes to open a door by hand, seconds.

Toughness float

How much battering a door opened by hand takes from the swarm before it's broken open.

BashPerSecond float

How much each swarmling at a shut door does to it a second.

BashReach float

How near a door's middle a swarmling batters it, meters.
