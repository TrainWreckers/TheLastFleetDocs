A weapon as designers and the loot generator see it: an archetype plus Destiny-style 0–100 stats, and three scales on the frame's own values that weapon parts can nudge (for example a coil barrel hits harder, a drum holds more, a scope zooms further). Parts and affixes adjust these, never the raw physical values.
