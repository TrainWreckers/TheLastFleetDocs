Parameters

Position Vector3

Eye position in world space, in meters.

Forward Vector3

Unit view direction. Must not be vertical (the simulation clamps pitch to ±89°).

VerticalFieldOfView float

Vertical field of view, in radians.

Shift Vector2

Where the view's center lands on screen, in normalized device units (−1 to 1, +Y up). Zero means the screen's center. A shifted view is the same view drawn off center without distortion. LastFleet Studio uses this to draw a picture of the sandbox in a corner.
