Remarks

The shapes are: a chamfered box (edges and corners cut at 45°), a prism (a round part with a few sides and cut ends), a tube, a rail with ridges, a row of vents (dark slots set into a face), a bolt's hexagonal head, and a block with any eight corners (a raked magazine, a tapered stock). Everything is flat-shaded, so every face has its own normal.

Box(ForgePart, Vector3, Vector3, string, float, Matrix4x4?)

Adds a box centered on center with half sizes half, rotated by turn around its center. With a chamfer, the four edges along its longest side are cut at 45°, giving an octagonal cross-section. That's the cut a machined part has, for about a third more triangles instead of twice as many.
