Remarks

The Director sends a patrol of soldiers out of the nearest closet, plus a horde if the mission's hordes are active (so nothing the level wouldn't have yet anyway). If the pod is held (a standing player within GuardRadius) until the drone lands, the drone takes it and its contents belong to the fleet (Delivered), even if the mission fails afterward. If it's left to the enemy for OverrunSeconds, it's overrun and the contents are lost.

This is one of the command systems and runs after the mission system (it reads and adds to the mission state). Placing and loading the pods is done by the simulation (the inventory, cargo and pickup systems). Doesn't allocate.
