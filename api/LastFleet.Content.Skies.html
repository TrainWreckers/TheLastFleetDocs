Namespace LastFleet.Content.Skies

Classes

SkyBand

A sky's dust band: the galaxy's disc seen edge on, a glowing band of stars and dust round the sky (SpaceSky).

SkyLibrary

The skies windows show (content/defs/skies.json): each a SpaceSky, and the order the fleet meets them in, one a sector (ForSector(int)), going round again past the last. Tuned in Studio's Look mode.

SkyNebula

A sky's nebula: clouds of glowing gas, shading from Near in their thick parts to Far at their edges (SpaceSky).

SkyStars

A sky's stars: how many, how bright, and their tint (SpaceSky).

SkySun

A sky's star: the sector's sun, a bright disc with a glow round it (SpaceSky).

SpaceSky

A sector's sky (content/defs/skies.json, SkyLibrary): the dark of space, its stars, a nebula, the galaxy's dust band and the sector's sun. Windows show it (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 9). Where in the sector the fleet is turns it (At(float, float, uint)), so the sun stands elsewhere at each system.

Structs

SkyLook

A sky as the renderer draws it, for one place: a SpaceSky turned for where in the sector the fleet is (At(float, float, uint)). Colors are linear and scaled by their strengths; directions are unit vectors in world space.
