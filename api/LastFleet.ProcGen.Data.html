Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Data

Classes

KindNames

The names the procgen data gives kinds of things (a hall's structure, floor and accents, a shape's kind): each name in camel case (columns, columnFaces, l), read back whatever its case.

ProcGenData

The designer data levels are generated from, read from content/defs/procgen: the content's tags (TagsFileName), surface styles (StylesFileName), room archetypes (ArchetypesFileName), the props (PropsFileName) and rules that place them (RulesFileName), the networks that join a level's things (NetworksFileName), where its loot goes (LootFileName), how its rooms are typed (TypingFileName), and the plans structure-first levels are built from (PlansFileName) with the rooms of their districts (DistrictsFileName).
