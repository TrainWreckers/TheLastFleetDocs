Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Components.Gear

Classes

ItemDefinition

What an item is like (content/defs/loot/items.json): its name, model and colors, how many stack in a slot, what it's for (Roles), and the fields that its roles turn on.

Items

The items currently in play (ItemDefinition). Once the loot data's items.json is put in play with Use(IReadOnlyList<ItemDefinition>?), those are used. Otherwise it's the shipped ones (Shipped), which match the repository's data. In play, an item is identified by its position in the list (ItemKind). What a consumable does is defined by its effect (EffectRegistry).

Structs

Container

A container to search, like a footlocker or a case, with its WorldPosition at the middle of its base. A player opens it by holding Interact, the same as picking up an item (see WeaponPickupSystem), and its contents are laid out on top as items to take. Once opened, it stays open.

ContainerItems

What's inside a Container.

Inventory

What a player carries besides their weapons (Loadout). There are four quick slots for consumables used in the field (medkits, ammo packs) and a pack of twelve slots for everything else, except items carried in both arms. Slots are numbered with the quick slots first (0 to 3), then the pack (4 to 15).

ItemDrop

Items lying in the world at their WorldPosition, resting on whatever is under them (a floor, a shelf). A player holding Interact next to them picks up whatever fits (see WeaponPickupSystem).

ItemKind

Identifies an item (other than weapons, which are carried in the Loadout) by its position in the items in play (Items, from the loot data's items.json), counting from 1. 0 means none.

ItemPile

A stack of one kind of item, either in an inventory slot or lying in the world.

LootDrop

Marks a character that may drop a weapon where it dies. The loot system only rolls for it once (Rolled).

PackSlots

The pack slots of an Inventory.

QuickSlots

The quick slots of an Inventory, for consumables used in the field.
