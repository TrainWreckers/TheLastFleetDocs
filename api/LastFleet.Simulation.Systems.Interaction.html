Works out what a player is looking at, for interacting with it. A thing counts as looked at when the line of sight from the eye passes within its radius (treating it as an upright column from its base up to its height) and it's close enough to reach.

Remarks

Picking things up, opening, turning, unloading, leaving on the dropship and starting the hack all use this. So standing next to something does nothing until the player turns to face it, and when two things are side by side, the one nearer the middle of the view is the one used.
