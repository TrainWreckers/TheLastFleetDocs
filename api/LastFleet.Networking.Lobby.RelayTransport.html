Wraps the lobby's connection for one mission. The mission's NetHost or NetClient talks to this as if it were the real connection, while the lobby keeps the real one open between missions.

Remarks

The lobby feeds in whatever arrives for the mission with Inject(NetEventKind, int, ReadOnlySpan<byte>). Anything the mission sends goes straight out on the real connection. When the mission is over, the lobby calls Detach(), and after that nothing the old mission does (sending, disconnecting players, disposing) reaches the connection.
