Remarks

That covers: hunger's health cost per shot; lifesteal, corrosion and arcs to the next enemy on a hit; restoration, a banked kill for hunger, and rounds back for a tolling precision kill on a kill; corrosion damage over time; and whether each weapon holder is holding the objective, for vigil. Damage multipliers are applied to the shot itself (WeaponTraitRules).

An arc, or a kill by corrosion, is added to the HitLog as its own hit, so hit markers, the kill feed and co-op clients treat it like any other hit. Doesn't allocate.
