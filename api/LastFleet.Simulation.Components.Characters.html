Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Components.Characters

Structs

Afflictions

Damage over time from a weapon trait. Corrosion (Corrosive) deals CorrosionPerSecond for CorrosionLeft seconds, credited to CorrosionSource. A fresh hit renews it instead of stacking.

CharacterBody

Links a character to its collision body in the ICollisionWorld.

EscapedTag

Marks a player who got off in an escape pod: sealed in it, out of the fight, waiting for the rest of the squad. The mission and the Director leave them out of the squad.

Faction

The faction a character belongs to.

Falling

Marks a loose object (a weapon, a pile, a case, a crate) that's falling because what it rested on broke (DropFallSystem). Holds its fall speed in m/s. It's removed when the object lands.

HandAction

What a character's hands are doing and how far along it is, as a client sees it for characters other than its own.

Health

Hit points for a character, with Destiny-style regeneration: after RegenDelaySeconds without damage, health refills at RegenPerSecond. A player's effects (PlayerEffects) can add a shield and armor in front of health, which absorb damage in that order, and can change how much damage it takes.

LinearVelocity

Linear velocity in meters per second.

MovementSettings

Movement tuning for a character, in meters and seconds. It's data, not code: roles, perks and implants change these values instead of changing the controller.

MovementState

Everything the player controller remembers between ticks.

Ping

A player's mark for the team (design doc: "ping system ... so pickup groups work without voice chat"). It marks an enemy or a place, and shows through walls with its distance until TimeLeft runs out. Each player gets one, so a new ping replaces the last. The entity's WorldPosition is where the mark is drawn.

PlayerCommand

One tick of player intent. This is the only way input reaches the simulation.

PlayerEffects

What effects are currently doing to a player (EffectSystem).

PlayerTag

Marks the entities controlled by players.

PuppetTag

Marks a character that's positioned from outside the simulation, like a co-op client's copies of the other players used for its prediction. Other characters bump into it, but the body system never moves it.

Rescue

A player's state for downed, revive and drag (see the design doc, "Downed, revive and drag").

ShieldGrant

A shield's size and how it regenerates (ShieldAction). An amount of zero means no shield.

SoldierTag

Marks AI-controlled soldiers.

SpawnPoint

Where a player respawns after dying.

ViewAngles

Where a character is looking, in radians.

WorldPosition

World-space position in meters. For characters this is the feet.

Enums

CommandButtons

Buttons held during a tick. Systems detect presses by comparing with the previous tick.

CoverKind

What kind of cover a character is behind, which decides how it peeks (CoverSystem).

FactionId

Which faction a character fights for. Shots never damage their own faction, but they're still stopped by it.

HandActionKind

What a character's hands are doing, so other players can see it (HandAction).

PingKind

What a ping marks.

Stance

Body posture. Sliding is a timed state entered from a sprint.
