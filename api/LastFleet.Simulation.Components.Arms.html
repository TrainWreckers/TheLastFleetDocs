Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Components.Arms

Structs

AmmoDrop

Marks a character that drops ammo where it dies.

AmmoPickup

Ammo lying in the world. Walking over it tops up every carried weapon's reserve by Magazines magazines' worth. A crate (RespawnSeconds above zero) respawns after a while. A drop disappears when it's taken or when LifetimeLeft runs out.

FireModeSet

A set of FireModes: the ones a frame allows, or the ones a trigger group can be switched between. Switching steps through them in FireMode's order (Automatic, SemiAutomatic, Burst, Manual), round to the first, whatever order the data lists them in.

FittedParts

The parts fitted to a weapon to replace the ones it rolled, one per part slot (barrel, magazine, stock, optic, module, muzzle, grip, trigger, in that order). An empty slot keeps the rolled part. This is part of a WeaponIdentity, so a weapon a player has modified still only takes a few bytes to send.

Grenade

A live grenade, in flight or resting (or a placed device), counting down to its explosion.

GrenadeBag

A character's grenades: how many it carries, and how they recharge.

Loadout

The weapons a player carries: two of them (a main weapon and a sidearm, or any two), with one in hand. Everything else they carry is in their Inventory.

PartFit

One weapon part as data: which part it is (the hash of its id in the loot data, see LastFleet.Loot) and how well its ranges rolled (0 is the worst of each range, 255 the best). A Part of zero means no part.

StatChange

A timed stat change from using a ChangeStatAction: which stat, by how much, and how many seconds are left.

StatChangeSlots

The stat changes a player has active (PlayerEffects). It's an inline array so the component stays a value type.

WeaponDrop

A weapon lying in the world with its ammo. It can come from a loot cache, a soldier's body, or a player who swapped it for another one. A player picks it up by holding Interact next to it (see WeaponPickupSystem).

WeaponFrame

The kind of gun, called its frame (for example an auto rifle or a shotgun), stored as its index in the frames in play (All, the loot data's archetypes in order). It's stored and sent as that index, so the host and clients must be using the same frames. The first frame is the default.

WeaponIdentity

Which loot roll a weapon came from: the seed, loot table and item level it's rebuilt from. That way a weapon only takes a few bytes to send, and the HUD can name it. Table is zero for a stock (unrolled) weapon.

WeaponSlot

A carried weapon that isn't in hand: its stats, plus its ammo and shot counter as they were when it was put away.

WeaponSlots

The slots of a Loadout.

WeaponState

Live state of an equipped weapon.

WeaponStats

Physical weapon values the simulation uses. WeaponStatsResolver produces them from the designer-facing 0–100 stats, so edit the profile, not these numbers.

Enums

FireMode

How the trigger turns into shots. A gun's comes from its trigger group (see FireModeSet).

WeaponTraits

What a weapon does beyond its numbers (see the design doc, "Manufacturers" and "Rarity and affixes"). A trait can be a manufacturer's signature, or the effect of an affix or a relic. The numbers live in WeaponTraitRules.
