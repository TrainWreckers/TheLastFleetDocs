Enum DoorKind

Namespace
LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Doors
Assembly
LastFleet.ProcGen.dll

What kind of door a shut door is, which sets how it looks. Each kind is recognizable from across a room.

public enum DoorKind : byte

Fields

Keycard = 0

A deck gate, opened by its keycard. Grey leaves with a red band and a keycard reader beside it.

Lockdown = 1

The gate into the last deck, sealed until the hack is done. Leaves striped yellow and black.

OneWay = 2

A one-way shortcut back across a gate. From the side it doesn't open from, its leaves show a no-entry sign. From the side it does open from, it has a green band and a release panel beside it.

Closet = 3

A spawn closet door that reinforcements come out of. Plain grey with a hazard kick plate.

Airlock = 4

The airlock's inner door, which opens once the lock has cycled. A heavy door in hazard paint.

Pressure = 5

A pressure door through a bulkhead, open until its compartment seals (BulkheadDoor). Grey leaves with a red seal band across them at the height of a hand and a white stripe under it.

Auto = 6

A room's door onto a hall (RoomDoorSpot) that slides open for whoever comes near and shuts behind them. Grey leaves with a lit strip across them and a dark window above.

Interact = 7

A room's door onto a hall that someone has to open (or shut) by hand: a player with Interact, a soldier on his way through; the swarm batters it open. Grey leaves with a handle at the seam and a kick plate.
