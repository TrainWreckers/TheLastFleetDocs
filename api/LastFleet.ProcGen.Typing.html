Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Typing

Classes

AdjacencyRule

Two rooms the mission joins (a room and the one it is reached from), one matching A and the other B, may never be: no lab next to a lab, say.

CountRule

A rule limiting how many rooms matching Where each deck, or the whole level, can have.

DistanceRule

A rule on how far apart rooms matching A are from rooms matching B, counted in rooms along the mission's paths. They're never closer than Min (for example two cargo holds kept well apart). If Max is set, each A room must also be within that many steps of some B room (for example a lab near the objective).

NodeTyping

Why one mission room is its type.

RoomTyper

Types the rooms of a mission (RoomTyping) before they're laid out. Each room becomes either one of the mission's archetypes or the kit's own, so the level's room mix follows the designers' rules (TypingRule).

RoomTyping

The result of typing a level's mission rooms (RoomTyper). Each room is either one of the mission's archetypes or the kit's own (null). It also records why: what each room could have been, at what weight, and which rule ruled out each type it didn't get.

RoomTypingException

Thrown when a level's rooms can't be typed under the rules (RoomTyper). The trace says why.

TypingData

How a level's rooms get typed (typing.json): how often a room is left to the kit's own rooms instead of being built from one of the mission's archetypes, and the rules the mix has to follow (TypingRule). If there's no typing.json, Default is used.

TypingRule

A rule a level's room mix keeps (typing.json), over each mission room's tags as its type would give them: its role (role.), its deck (deck.), whether it is on the main path (layout.main_path), and its type's (an archetype's content tags, or type.kit for a room of the kit's own).
