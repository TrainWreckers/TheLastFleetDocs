Add(Pose, Pose, float, ReadOnlySpan<float>)

Adds the difference between additive and reference to this pose, scaled by amount. Used for a lean, an aim offset or a kick authored as a clip from a neutral pose, on top of whatever the body is doing. With mask, each joint only gets as much as its weight.

Blend(Pose, float, ReadOnlySpan<float>)

Moves this pose amount of the way toward other (0 is none, 1 is all of it). Used to blend two clips or fade from one to the next. With mask (one weight per joint, from Mask(IEnumerable<string>)), each joint only moves as far as its weight allows, for layering such as a reload over the legs running.

Skin(in Matrix4x4, Span<Matrix4x4>)

Writes the skinning matrices for this pose into matrices (one per joint) for the GPU, with the model placed in the world by placement. Each joint's matrix is its inverse bind, then its pose, then the placement. A skinned vertex is the weighted sum of its joints' matrices applied to it.
