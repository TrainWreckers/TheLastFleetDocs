Headless, so it's tested like the rest of the generator.

Remarks

Its Edges are the collider's outline: the edges between faces that meet at an angle, and any edge with a face on one side only. Edges between flat neighbors (a quad's diagonal) are left out, so a mesh reads as its shape rather than its triangles.

Its warnings:

  • Walkable surfaces (no steeper than a character's slope limit) higher than the model's base but no higher than a character steps up, with room above them for a crouching character: walking into it, a character steps onto them (a pillar's plinth, a kiosk's raised floor). Surfaces with something solid just above them (a plinth's top inside the body standing on it, a footrest under a desk) and lips too small to stand on don't count.
  • For a mesh, edges with a face on one side only (a hole), or whose two faces wind the same way (one of them faces in): rays only hit a face from the front, so a shot or a line of sight can pass through there. An open edge lying along the model's base doesn't count: the floor closes it.
  • A piece that breaks given a mesh: it keeps its authored box (FurnitureModel).
