A level's rooms, as the world shaders use them for lighting. Holds a grid saying which room each cell belongs to (the station's own layout grid, where rooms are made of whole cells), and each room's bounce light for this frame: the light from its active lights, bounced once off its walls.

Remarks

The shaders look up a point's room in the grid and use its bounce as fill light: from above (Upper, on upward-facing surfaces) and from below (Lower, on downward-facing surfaces). Points outside any room get none. Each room's box is what its reflections are projected onto (ReflectionCapture), and its cube map is captured from CapturePoint(int). The grid and boxes are set once per level. The client writes the bounce every frame.

Remarks

With ray tracing, a light reaches into the rooms next to its own. The world shaders only count it at a point in the next room when the line from the point to the light passes through a doorway between the two rooms (throughDoorway in scene_lights.slang). That's what a shadow ray would find, minus the light's shading.
