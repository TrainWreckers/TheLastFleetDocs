Namespace LastFleet.Loot.Definitions

Classes

Affix

A prefix ("Overclocked") or suffix ("of the Vigil") that can roll onto weapons of high enough rarity.

Fabrication

Something the armory can fabricate from graded material (the "fabrication" data). It's either a weapon rolled from a loot table or a weapon part (for one slot or any slot). The item level comes from the grade it costs.

ItemTable

What a pile, container or cargo crate holds (content/defs/loot/items.json, "itemTables"). A roll picks one entry by weight, plus a count.

ItemTableEntry

One entry in an ItemTable: an item, how likely it is to be picked, and how many drop.

LootArchetype

A weapon frame for loot rolls: the simulation's WeaponArchetype, the 0–100 stats a roll starts from, and the bounds every stat gets clamped to at the end.

LootEntry

One weighted entry in a loot table. It can only drop between its min and max item levels.

LootTable

A loot table: weighted entries (archetypes, nested tables, relics), plus biases on the rarity and manufacturer odds. The biases multiply down through nested tables.

Manufacturer

A weapon manufacturer: its stat bias, which applies to every gun it makes, and its model names per archetype. A manufacturer with no model for an archetype doesn't make that archetype.

MaterialGrade

A grade of refined material (the "grades" data: Mk I, Mk II, ...). The fleet's refinery makes it from raw materials, and the armory uses it to fabricate items. A higher grade gives a higher item level.

Rarity

A rarity tier. Its chance is Weight × (1 + PerLevel × (item level − 1)), times the loot table's bias. So higher item levels shift the odds toward rarer tiers.

RefineryRecipe

A refinery recipe for turning raw materials into a material grade (the "recipes" data). One batch uses Inputs and produces Count of Grade.

RefineryTier

One upgrade level of the fleet's refinery (the "refineryTiers" data, lowest first). A fleet starts at the first tier. Each tier has an upgrade cost paid from the fleet's stores, the grades it can make, and how many batches it refines per jump.

Relic

A hand-authored unique weapon with a fixed archetype, manufacturer, parts and affixes. Every range rolls at its best value.

ReworkRules

How the armory's workbench reworks what's in the locker (crafting.json's "rework"), paid in refined material. A weapon is reworked to a higher item level: the same roll, its stats at the new level, paid in the grade that makes things of that level. A part is tuned: its ranges roll better, paid in the grade of its rarity.

SalvageYield

What the armory's workbench gets from dismantling a weapon or a part of one rarity (crafting.json's "salvage"): raw materials, by item id, which go to the fleet's stores for the refinery.

WeaponPart

A weapon part in a slot's pool.

Structs

LootColor

A HUD color, parsed from a #rrggbb value in the data.

StatRange

One stat change and the range it rolls in. If Multiply is false, it adds whole points to a 0–100 stat. If it's true, it multiplies the stat or scale. Additions are applied before multiplications.

Enums

FabricationKind

What a Fabrication makes.

LootEntryKind

What a loot table entry drops.

LootStat

A stat that a weapon part, manufacturer or affix can change. The first five are 0–100 stats. The rest are scales on the frame's damage, magazine or zoom (see WeaponProfile).

WeaponPartSlot

The part slots on a weapon (see the design doc, "Weapon anatomy"). The first five are the original slots. Muzzle, grip and trigger were added later. They roll after everything else, each from its own random stream, so adding them didn't change any weapon rolled before (see Since).
