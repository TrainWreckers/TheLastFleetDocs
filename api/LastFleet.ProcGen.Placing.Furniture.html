Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Furniture

Classes

Attachment

Extra furniture placed with a piece at its model's Attach_<Group>_<n> empties (the attach field in props.json), for example chairs around a table or a stool at a counter. Each one is placed with its origin at the empty, facing the piece, either tucked in or pulled out, and rotated a little. It's placed and checked together with the piece.

ColliderReport

What a model is collided with, as furniture (For(LibraryModel, ColliderKind, bool, float, float, float)) or a station fixture (ForFixture(LibraryModel, IReadOnlyList<string>, ColliderKind, float, float, float)), with a given ColliderKind, built exactly as the generator sets it into the level, and what's worth knowing about it: Studio's Models mode shows it over the model. Headless, so it's tested like the rest of the generator.

Furnishing

A furnishing style for built rooms, from the furnishings in rules.json (offices, a warehouse, and so on). It holds the rules used to place furniture while the room is built. A room archetype names the furnishing it uses; a room without one picks one at random.

FurnitureModel

A furniture model (a top-level object in a file under content/props, named by its prop's model), as the generator builds it into a room: its footprint, its solid boxes, its visible shape, and the spots on it where items go (Sockets) and where other furniture attaches (Attachments). Its origin is the center of its base, and it faces into the room along its forward axis (glTF -Z, Blender +Y), the same as a station fixture.

SocketLoot

The loot a group of sockets on a furniture model holds (the sockets field in props.json). Once a designer gives a group loot, the group belongs to them, so the placement rules don't put anything on it. Which of its sockets get filled is rolled with the level.

Structs

ColliderEdge

An edge of a collider's outline, in the placed model's space (Mesh).

FurnitureLoot

A socket group on a placed piece that a designer gave loot to (SocketLoot), with the positions of its reachable sockets. Positions are in room space while the room is built, and in level space after. Which sockets get filled, and with what, is rolled with the level (LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Supplies.FurnitureLootRolls).

FurnitureSocket

A marked point on a furniture model (FurnitureModel), in model space. It's either a spot where an item sits (Socket_Top_1) or where another piece attaches (Attach_Seat_1).
