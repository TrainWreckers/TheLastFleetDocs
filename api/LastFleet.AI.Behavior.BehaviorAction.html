Enum BehaviorAction

Namespace
LastFleet.AI.Behavior
Assembly
LastFleet.AI.dll

The actions a soldier can perform, each one a leaf of its behavior tree. The game decides how each is done (the soldier's movement, aim and tactics); the tree decides when.

public enum BehaviorAction : byte

Fields

Roam = 0

Walk its patrol route, or look around where it's standing. Always succeeds.

WatchEnemy = 1

Turn to face where the enemy was noticed while it reacts. Always succeeds.

ChooseIntent = 2

Score hold, advance, push and fall back (using the behavior's scoring) and take the best one as its intent. Always succeeds.

FightFromCover = 3

Fight from cover according to its intent: hold, move up, fall back or push, all in one action. Always succeeds. The shipped tree uses HoldCover, BoundForward, PushEnemy and FallBack instead, so each one shows up separately.

FightInOpen = 4

Fight in the open: aim, fire in bursts, stay in its range band, strafe. Always succeeds.

ClearRooms = 5

Clear the rooms between it and the enemy together with its squad, stacking up at doors. Fails if there are no rooms to clear.

GoToEnemy = 6

Go to the enemy's last known position. Always succeeds.

LookAround = 7

Stand still and look around for the enemy. Always succeeds.

TopUpMagazine = 8

Reload when there's nobody to shoot and the magazine is low. Always succeeds.

ThrowGrenade = 9

Throw a grenade where the enemy is hiding, if the squad is allowed to and the throw is clear. Always succeeds.

DodgeGrenades = 10

Run from grenades landing nearby. Always succeeds.

HoldCover = 11

Stay in its cover (or take the best one nearby), peek out and fire, and duck when hit. A flanker takes cover around the side. Always succeeds (it fights in the open if there's no cover).

BoundForward = 12

Bound forward with the squad. The soldier farthest back moves first, to cover near or in front of the squad's lead, while the rest cover it, and nobody gets farther ahead than the squad's cohesion allows. Succeeds while it bounds (or holds the cover it bounded to). Fails if it isn't its turn or there's no cover ahead to bound to.

PushEnemy = 13

Leave cover and close in on the enemy, firing. Always succeeds.

FallBack = 14

Fall back to cover farther from the enemy, one bound at a time. Always succeeds.

SearchForEnemy = 15

Search for a lost enemy where he most likely went: past the doorways he was heading for (out to the node's value in rooms, 2 if not set), straight ahead along his path, and where he was last seen. The squad splits the guesses up and each soldier checks the most likely one near it. Fails once every guess has been checked.
