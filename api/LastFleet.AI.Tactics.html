Namespace LastFleet.AI.Tactics

Classes

CoverMap

Cover points found in the level once, at load time: walkable spots next to walls and obstacles, tested with horizontal rays at crouching and standing height.

LightMap

Tracks where the station is dark, so soldiers know when to turn on their flashlights. Holds each room's lights, minus the ones that are dead or shot out. A point counts as dark when its room's working lights barely reach it.

RoomMap

A generated level's rooms and the doorways between them, so soldiers can fight indoors. Answers which room a point is in, which doorways lead out of a room, and the route from room to room.

TacticalUtility

Utility scoring for what a soldier in a fight does next: hold its cover, advance, push the enemy, or fall back. Uses the shipped data (Default, content/defs/ai/soldier.json) with the first personality, a steady squad.

Structs

CoverMap.Nearby

Enumerates the points in the squares around a spot, square by square. See Near(Vector3, float).

CoverPoint

A place to take cover: a walkable spot with something solid on one side.

RoomCells

A room's cells on the layout grid: the corner and size of its bounding box, its lowest story and how many stories it spans. For a room that doesn't fill its box (an L or U shape), it also lists the cells it does fill.

RoomDoor

A doorway between two rooms. Holds the doorway's center at floor level and the horizontal direction from room A into room B.

RoomLight

A working light in a room, for LightMap.
