Remarks

The hose is laid out wherever its holder walks, and walking back along it picks it up again. The holder carries it like a crate: slower, no sprint, weapon lowered. Brought to the dropship's port, it couples and starts pumping. At its full length it's pulled out of the holder's hands. Drag (F) puts it down, Fire drops it to fight, and it also drops if the holder is downed or dies. Picking up a tank's hose (or one lying on the ground) and repairing a cut are handled by the pickup system (Take(ref SupplyTank, int, Vector3, Entity), hold Interact).

Runs after the cargo system (which lets a player without a crate move freely) and before movement. Doesn't allocate.
