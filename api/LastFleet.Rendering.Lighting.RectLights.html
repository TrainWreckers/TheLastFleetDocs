Class RectLights

Namespace
LastFleet.Rendering.Lighting
Assembly
LastFleet.Rendering.dll

Computes the light a rectangle casts on a surface, the same way the world shader does (rectIrradiance in content/shaders/scene_lights.slang). It uses Lambert's formula for the rectangle's polygon as seen from the surface, after clipping it at the surface's horizon. This is for tests and tools; the shader is what actually lights the frame.

FarFactor

How far away a rect light has to be, in multiples of its half diagonal, before the world shader treats it as a point light (FarRect). At that distance the result is within a few percent of the full polygon's.

FarIrradiance(Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, Vector3)

Returns the irradiance a small rectangle of unit radiance casts on a surface, treated as a point: its area times the cosines at both ends, divided by the squared distance between their centers. The shader uses this past FarFactor.

Irradiance(Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, Vector3)

Returns the irradiance a rectangle of unit radiance, centered at center with half axes halfU and halfV, casts on a surface at position facing normal. The result is 0 to pi, where pi means it fills the surface's whole sky.
