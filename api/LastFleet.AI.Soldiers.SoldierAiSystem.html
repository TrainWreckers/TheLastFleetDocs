Remarks

Runs first in the tick, so soldiers act on what the world looked like at the end of the last tick, exactly as a player does. Everything a soldier knows it saw, heard or was told: no perfect information.

Deterministic: random choices hash the soldier's seed with a draw counter. Allocation-free after warm-up.
