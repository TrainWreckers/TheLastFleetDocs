Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Components.World

Structs

Destructible

A level part that can be destroyed. Shots and grenades wear it down. When it breaks, its collision is turned off (so it no longer blocks movement, sight or shots), and a BreakRecord tells the client to hide it and throw debris.

HullPull

The pull toward a breached window (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10): air rushing out through it, dragging anyone who can see it toward it, harder the nearer they are (HullPulls). The host's breach system makes one while a breach is open; a client's prediction gets a copy from the mission's state, so the pull is felt without waiting on the host.

Target

A shootable practice target made of level surfaces: a body, and a head that takes precision damage. It drops when its health runs out, and stands back up fully healed after RespawnSeconds.
