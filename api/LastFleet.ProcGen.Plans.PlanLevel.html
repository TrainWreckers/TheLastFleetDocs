Remarks

Plots are the cells between halls, in one section. Each is cut across its length into strips, so the rooms' walls line up along the hall, each strip a room picked by weight from its district (DistrictProgram), and the section's biggest plot holds its anchor. A strip deeper than MostDepth becomes a room in front and one behind; anything smaller than LeastRoom cells is left as void inside the hull.

Each room's door faces the hall it has the most frontage on. A room between two halls usually gets a second door onto the other if it's shared (a mess, a lab), rarely if it's private (a cabin, a store). A room with no hall of its own gets a suite door through the room in front of it. Halls are built in stretches at most 8 cells each way, joined by openings as wide as the hall; pressure doors and hatches come from the structure.

Every piece's doors are known before it's built, so no room has a doorway nothing uses. Identical pieces (a row of cabins, a stretch of corridor) are built once: a piece's seed comes from its archetype, shape and doors, and built pieces are kept for each set of procgen data.
