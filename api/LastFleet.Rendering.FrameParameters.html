Parameters

ClearColor Vector4

Linear RGBA color the frame is cleared to. Distant geometry also fades to this color.

Camera CameraView

The camera the frame is drawn from.

FogDensity float

How quickly distant geometry fades to ClearColor, per meter.

Sun float

Sun strength (a warm key light from high up): 1 outdoors, 0 inside a station.

Ambient float

Strength of the sky-and-ground fill light under everything.

Exposure float

Multiplier on scene light before the tone curve (1 leaves it unchanged).

Scope ScopeLens

The scope lens drawn over the frame, if looking through a scope (none by default).
