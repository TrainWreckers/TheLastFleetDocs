Namespace LastFleet.AI.Utility

Classes

IntentRule

How one intent gets scored (UtilityModel).

Personality

A squad's personality: a scale on each intent's score (a bold squad pushes more, a cautious one falls back sooner), plus how often squads get it.

UtilityFactor

A multiplier applied to an intent's score after its terms are added up. The value comes from its curve and/or its value for the soldier's role (1 for a role that isn't listed). It only applies when its conditions hold.

UtilityModel

How soldiers decide what to do in a fight (a behavior's scoring, content/defs/ai/soldier.json): utility scoring of hold, advance, push and fall back. Each intent's score is its base plus its terms, times its factors (IntentRule), times the squad personality's scale for it. The highest score wins, with the current intent getting Stickiness added on top.

UtilityTerm

A value added to an intent's score: Weight times its curve (1 if there's no curve) times its weight for the soldier's role (1 if there are no role weights, 0 for a role that isn't listed). It only applies when its conditions hold.
