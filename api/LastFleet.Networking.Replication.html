Namespace LastFleet.Networking.Replication

Classes

CharacterView

What one client is told about where each character is, kept on the host.

EventBuffer

Events received from the host, kept until the presentation processes them.

EventCodec

Encodes shots and hits for the wire. They're packed down to just what a client needs to draw and play them, about a fifth of their size in memory.

ReplicaWorld

A client's copy of the host's world, built from snapshots. It has the same entities, with the host's ids, and the components the presentation reads (characters, grenades, pickups, dropped weapons, targets).

SnapshotDelta

Delta compression for snapshots. Each snapshot is sent as a delta from one the client has acknowledged. Its bytes are XORed with the baseline's, so anything unchanged becomes zero, and the result is sent as runs: a count of unchanged bytes, then a count of changed bytes followed by those bytes, and so on.

SnapshotHistory

The last few snapshots sent to one client (on the host) or received by it (on the client), stored by tick. These are the baselines a delta can be encoded against and decoded from.

SnapshotWriter

Writes the host's world as a snapshot for one client: every character's pose and visible state, the client's own player in full (movement, weapons, loadout, grenades), grenades, pickups, dropped weapons, broken props, practice targets, and the horde near the client (SwarmView).

SwarmView

What one client knows about the horde, kept on the host, and the horde's part of that client's snapshots. It stays within the design's budget (64 kbps for everything) no matter how many swarmlings there are.

Structs

TickEvents

One tick's events, in the form the presentation needs: shots (tracers, muzzle flashes), hits (markers, impacts, damage indicators), explosions, broken props, barks, and effect sounds and flashes. On the host these come from the simulation's logs. On a client they're what the host sent.
