Remarks

The first mission has no horde. Starting from a mission set by the Difficulty, the Choir shows up, at first only once the hack has started. Each extraction after that brings it earlier: from the objective's deck, then the deck before that, and so on back to the start, and after that sooner, more often and bigger. Every extraction also makes the Director quicker to send patrols and shortens its breathers.
