The weapon parts' recipes (content/weapons/forge.json; docs/plans/weapons.md phase 5): for each part file (a frame's, and shared), each part's shapes and markers, which the forge builds into the part files the game reads (ForgeFile). A recipe's shapes, each one object naming its kind by its first key:

  • box: its center; size (whole, not half); bevel its edges' cut; turn degrees about x, y, z.
  • prism: one end, to the other; r its radius; sides (8); bevel.
  • tube: one end, to the other; r outside, inner inside; sides (12).
  • rail: one end, to the other; width; up its top's way ([0, 1, 0]); pitch its ridges'.
  • vents: the first's center; along the row's way; facing the face's; count; step; size each (along, deep, across).
  • bolt: its head's center; facing; r.
  • block: its eight corners, as a box's (x fastest, then y, then z).

Each takes m, its material (Materials; Body by default), and detail (true for the small things only your own gun shows: Detail). A part's markers name points: its sockets, hands' places, muzzle, sight.

Remarks

The props' recipes (content/props/forge.json, docs/plans/furniture.md) are the same with two things more: their own materials (each a name, a color and its surface in the library), named in place of the weapon kit's, and each part's collision, the boxes that are solid of it (each a box, its center, and its size), its shapes then drawn only. Props have no far version nor detail.
