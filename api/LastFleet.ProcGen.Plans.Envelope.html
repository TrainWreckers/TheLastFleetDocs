The outline of a level on one deck: the grid cells inside its hull, built from a plan's parts for a seed. Everything later in the structure-first pipeline (halls, sections, rooms) happens inside it, and its hull edges are where the level meets space.

Remarks

Held as a grid of flags over its bounds. The same plan and seed always make the same envelope, on every machine: each part draws from its own stream (Envelope, keyed by the part's index), so changing one part leaves the others' draws alone.

Build(LevelPlan, ulong, int)

Builds plan's envelope on deck for seed: each of the deck's parts in order, its cells added or cut out. The bounds are those of the cells added, kept within MostSpan. A part draws from its own stream whether it's on the deck or not, so a part comes out the same on every deck it's on.
