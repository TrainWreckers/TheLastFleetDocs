What a player carries besides their weapons (Loadout). There are four quick slots for consumables used in the field (medkits, ammo packs) and a pack of twelve slots for everything else, except items carried in both arms. Slots are numbered with the quick slots first (0 to 3), then the pack (4 to 15).
