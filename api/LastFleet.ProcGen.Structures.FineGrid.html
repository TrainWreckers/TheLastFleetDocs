One story of a room at CellSize resolution: which half-meter squares of its floor are inside the room. Shapes finer than the layout's 4 m grid (chamfered corners, insets, pillars) get carved out here, and walls, floors and ceilings are found from its edges.

Remarks

Coordinates are local to the room: fine cell (0, 0) is the square at the room's corner, with X along its width and Z along its depth, same as Footprint. Using integer cells keeps every decision exact and identical on every machine.
