The items currently in play (ItemDefinition). Once the loot data's items.json is put in play with Use(IReadOnlyList<ItemDefinition>?), those are used. Otherwise it's the shipped ones (Shipped), which match the repository's data. In play, an item is identified by its position in the list (ItemKind). What a consumable does is defined by its effect (EffectRegistry).
