SpawnTank(EntityStore, Vector3, ItemKind, int, Vector3, float, float, int, Vector3?, IReadOnlyList<PipeValve>?, float, (Vector3 Bottom, Vector3 Top)?)

Spawns a supply tank with its valve at valve. The tank itself is part of the level, with its center at center.

public static Entity SpawnTank(EntityStore store, Vector3 valve, ItemKind supply, int amount, Vector3 port, float hoseLength, float rate, int capacity = 0, Vector3? center = null, IReadOnlyList<PipeValve>? valves = null, float valveSeconds = 3, (Vector3 Bottom, Vector3 Top)? gauge = null)

Returns

Entity

Remarks

It holds amount out of capacity (0 means full). Its hose is hoseLength meters long and pumps rate per second into the dropship's port. If it starts with nothing in it, it's empty from the start. With valves, it's the outlet of a deep reservoir and stays sealed until each valve is opened (by holding for valveSeconds). The gauge window runs from gauge's bottom to its top. Without gauge there's no gauge.
