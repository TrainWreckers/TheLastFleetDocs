Class LifeSystem

Namespace
LastFleet.Simulation.Systems

Handles health over time: regeneration, being downed, death and respawn. A dead character's input is ignored and its collision body is removed, so others can walk through the corpse. Players with a SpawnPoint respawn after RespawnSeconds with the stock kit (DefaultLoadout(ulong)). The weapons they found and everything in their inventory are left where they died, for them or a teammate to pick up. Soldiers stay dead.

Remarks

A player (with Rescue) who hits zero health while a teammate is still up gets downed instead. They bleed out over BleedOutSeconds, can only look, fire a sidearm, reload and ping (DownedCommand(in PlayerCommand, in Loadout)), and only die if nobody revives them in time (RescueSystem). A player on their own dies right away, as before.

Runs first among the gameplay systems, but after any system that writes commands (the AI), so it can cancel them.
