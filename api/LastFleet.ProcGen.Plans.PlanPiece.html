One piece of a plan's level, built as one room module: a room cut from a plot, or a stretch of hall. Long halls are built in stretches at most 8 cells each way, joined by openings as wide as the hall.

Rotation int

Quarter turns it's built at (a stair core, built climbing north and turned to climb its way); 0 for anything else.

Foot Side

A stair core's foot door, as it's built climbing north: on the south side of its last row, or the west or east.

Head int

A stair core's landing door, as it's built climbing north: which cell of its first row's north side.
