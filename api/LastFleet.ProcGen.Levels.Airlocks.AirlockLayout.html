The middle of the outer hatch, on the floor. This is where the dropship docks.

Outward Vector3

The direction out through the hatch, horizontal and unit length.

Port Vector3

The dropship's intake beside the hatch, on the floor in front of it. This is where a tank's hose gets coupled (see TankSpot).

Chute Vector3

The dropship's cargo chute in the hull on the other side of the hatch, at its base. This is where the fleet's supplies get unloaded into the hold (as seen from in front of it).
