Builds the airlock into the insertion bay: a chamber around the player spawns against the bay's wall that has no doorway (the hull), the outer hatch in that wall where the dropship is docked, and an inner wall with a door the mission opens once the lock has cycled (AirlockLayout).

HullSide(RoomModule)

Returns the side of an insertion module the airlock backs onto (the hull, where the dropship docks). That's the side it was built against (Hull) if it knows, otherwise the first of south, north, east and west with no doorway, in the module's own frame, or null if every side has one. The layout keeps the space beyond it empty.
