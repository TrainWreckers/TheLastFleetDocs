The surface materials every model is drawn with (content/defs/materials.json). It's a fixed library, tuned in Studio and picked in Blender, so paint looks like paint on every gun, prop and wall. Each vertex stores its material's index into this library (Material), and the renderer reads the library as a table.

Remarks

A model's material names its library material in its glTF extras (ExtrasKey, a Blender custom property; LegacyExtrasKey in older exports). If it doesn't, it falls back based on its name (FallbackId(string?, bool)): a material called Glass gets glass, Light and anything that glows gets emissive, a Paint_ slot gets painted metal, and everything else gets the first material (plastic).
