A furniture model (a top-level object in a file under content/props, named by its prop's model), as the generator builds it into a room: its footprint, its solid boxes, its visible shape, and the spots on it where items go (Sockets) and where other furniture attaches (Attachments). Its origin is the center of its base, and it faces into the room along its forward axis (glTF -Z, Blender +Y), the same as a station fixture.

Remarks

Each mesh object under it is visible, solid, or both, based on its name, the same as a station fixture (docs/blender/props.md). One named ..._NoCollision is visible only, one named ..._CollisionOnly is solid only, and anything else is both.

What it's collided with is its Collider (content/props/colliders.json). Placed, the part named after the piece is what's solid (and what damage goes through, for a breakable piece), and anything visible apart from it is one more part, name/Shape, which belongs to it (BreakableLayout):

  • Authored: each solid box (which has to be a box) is its own part, the first named after the piece and the rest name/Solid2 and up.
  • Mesh: what's drawn is solid as it is, one part named after the piece. The file's solid boxes are left out.
  • Box and Cylinder: one solid part around what's drawn, named after the piece.
  • None: only what's drawn.

A piece that breaks takes damage through one solid part, which a mesh isn't (shots hit what's drawn, not the part named after it): it keeps its authored box instead.
