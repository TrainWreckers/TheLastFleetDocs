Frag grenades. Handles throwing them (a fresh press of Grenade throws along the view), flying and bouncing off the level, and exploding when the fuse runs out. Explosions damage the thrower's enemies if there's a clear line from the blast, with damage falling off with distance, and are recorded in the ExplosionLog.

Remarks

The long fuse is the warning: there's time to see a grenade land and get away from it. Launcher rounds (shots with a ProjectileSpeed, read from the ShotLog after the weapon system runs) fly the same way under a third of the gravity, and burst on the first wall, floor or character they hit.
