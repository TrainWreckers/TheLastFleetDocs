Namespace LastFleet.Content.Forge

Classes

ForgeFile

Writes forged parts (ForgePart) out as a weapon part file (content/weapons/*.gltf, see docs/blender/weapons.md), in the same layout the Blender add-on and the greybox kit used.

ForgePart

A weapon part as the forge builds it (docs/plans/weapons.md phase 5). It has the name the game looks for (Receiver, Barrel_0_Plain), its triangles in its own space (with the origin at the attach point), and its markers. ForgeShapes adds shapes to it, and ForgeFile writes parts out as glTF.

ForgeRecipes

The weapon parts' recipes (content/weapons/forge.json; docs/plans/weapons.md phase 5): for each part file (a frame's, and shared), each part's shapes and markers, which the forge builds into the part files the game reads (ForgeFile). A recipe's shapes, each one object naming its kind by its first key:

  • box: its center; size (whole, not half); bevel its edges' cut; turn degrees about x, y, z.
  • prism: one end, to the other; r its radius; sides (8); bevel.
  • tube: one end, to the other; r outside, inner inside; sides (12).
  • rail: one end, to the other; width; up its top's way ([0, 1, 0]); pitch its ridges'.
  • vents: the first's center; along the row's way; facing the face's; count; step; size each (along, deep, across).
  • bolt: its head's center; facing; r.
  • block: its eight corners, as a box's (x fastest, then y, then z).

Each takes m, its material (Materials; Body by default), and detail (true for the small things only your own gun shows: Detail). A part's markers name points: its sockets, hands' places, muzzle, sight.

ForgeShapes

The shapes forged parts are built from (docs/plans/weapons.md phase 5). These are what make low-poly parts look manufactured instead of just boxy.

Structs

ForgeMarker

A named empty on a forged part (ForgePart), for example a socket, a hand position, a muzzle or a sight.

ForgeMaterial

A material used by a forged part (Materials). It has a preview color and a surface material from the library (content/defs/materials.json).

ForgeTriangle

One triangle of a forged part (ForgePart), wound counter-clockwise when seen from outside.
