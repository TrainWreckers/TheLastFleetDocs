A hall along a part's line: a ship's spine, a passageway either side of it, a station arm's corridor, a bridge. It runs from From to To of the part's length (shares that may go past 0 and 1 to reach into the parts beside it), at Offset cells across, and stops where the envelope ends or leaves fewer than Inset cells either side of it for rooms. Only its longest stretch is kept, so it never breaks in two.

What it is.

On int

The part it's laid on.

Width int

How many cells wide it is.

Offset int

How many cells across from the part's line it runs: +1 is one cell right of it looking along it (south of an eastward line). With FromEdge, counted in from the part's side instead: 1 is the outermost row on the side its sign says.

FromEdge bool

Whether the offset is counted in from the part's side rather than out from its line.

Mirror bool

Whether a second one runs at the opposite offset.

From float

Where it starts, as a share of the part's length (0 its start).

To float

Where it ends, as a share of the part's length (1 its end).

Inset int

The fewest cells inside the envelope it keeps either side of it.

Meet bool

Whether it reaches past its ends to meet a hall carved before it (within MeetReach cells), stopping where it does: a bridge running on to the rings it joins.

MinLength float

The shortest it may be, as a share of the part's length; shorter, and it's left out (a passageway only a sponson has room for).
