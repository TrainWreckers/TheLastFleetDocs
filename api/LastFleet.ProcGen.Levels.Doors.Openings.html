Class Openings

Namespace
LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Doors
Assembly
LastFleet.ProcGen.dll

Handles doorway openings in the level. Where two modules' openings meet, the passage is the stretch both are open over. Whatever one opening has beyond the other (for example a wide hall's end against a kit room's 2 m doorway) is walled up in that module's own wall. An unused doorway is walled up all the way across.
