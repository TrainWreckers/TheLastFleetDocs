Resolves your own shots on this machine the moment they're fired, against the level's collision and against the other characters and the horde where they're drawn. A hit on an enemy shows a hit marker right away, a full round trip before the host confirms it.

Remarks

This matches what the host does: it rewinds characters to the same place using the render tick sent with each command, and it knows where this client draws each swarmling.

It's only a prediction; the host's hits are what count. Damage is the shot's damage at that distance, and a kill is predicted when the damage is at least the health the host last sent. Hits on teammates and practice targets are left to the host. Doesn't allocate after the first few shots.
