Namespace LastFleet.AI.Fleet

Classes

CampaignTarget

Something the fleet can raid in a star system (content/defs/campaign.json): a station, or a ship of one of the plans.

CampaignTuning

What the fleet finds to raid as it crosses the sectors (content/defs/campaign.json): stations, laid out by the station plans, and ships (frigates, carriers, strike carriers), each laid out by its plan, each turning up as often as its weight once the fleet has come far enough. Each system has a few of them (its points of interest), and a sector's first system and its gate always have a station first. A point raided is picked over: its cargo thinner, its guards heavier.

FleetNeed

One line on the needs board: a supply, how many jumps the fleet's stock of it will last, and the text shown.

FleetShip

One ship in the fleet: its kind, its name, how many people are aboard, and its condition (0 is lost, 100 is undamaged).

FleetSim

The fleet's simulation between missions (design doc, "The Fleet: home base" and "The Scourge at the strategic layer").

FleetState

The fleet's state between missions (design doc, "The Fleet: home base"): its ships and people, its stock of each supply, its morale, how many missions and jumps it has made, and how far behind the Scourge is.

FleetVote

The squad's votes at the star map (design doc, "Co-op design": voting at the star map). Everyone picks a course, and the course is the one most of them picked. Everyone can vote to jump, and the fleet jumps once more than half the squad has. The host breaks ties: a course they picked wins a tie, and their vote to jump carries a split squad.

Galaxy

The galaxy the fleet is running across (design doc, "Vast to look at, few choices to make"): a seeded spiral of stars, with every sector a region of it. The fleet starts near the core and runs outward along an arm, a sector at a time, so its trail leads back across everything it's crossed.

JumpTuning

How the fleet's jump plays out on the ship (campaign.json's jump): the countdown once the squad has voted, how far the lights go down while the drive spools up, and the flash and shake as it jumps.

LockerItem

A weapon or a loose part in the armory's locker (design doc, "The armoury"). The locker is the fleet's, so it's everyone's: kept between missions, and anyone on the ship can take from it or put into it.

PointOfInterest

Somewhere in a star system the squad can drop to (design doc, "Jumping and points of interest"): a station, or a ship of one of the plans. Holds its seed and the settings it's generated with: how heavily it's guarded and what its cargo is mostly made of.

StarMap

Builds and queries the sector the fleet is crossing (design doc, "Map structure"). A sector has ten to fourteen systems from its entry (on the left) to its gate (on the right). Each is linked to its nearest neighbors so there are alternate routes, and each has a few points of interest to drop to, stations and ships.

StarSystem

A star system in the sector the fleet is crossing (design doc, "Star map"). Holds its map position, its lanes, and its points of interest: the stations and ships there the squad can drop to.
