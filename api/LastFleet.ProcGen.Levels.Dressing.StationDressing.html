Dresses the placed rooms so they look like a space station instead of boxes under the sky: a ceiling over every room (where no room is above it), light panels in the ceiling, and the lights they cast, colored by the room's role. A few panels are dead and a few flicker.

Remarks

A ceiling is a one-sided face pointing down. Rays from below (shots, grenades, a character's head) hit it. A ray from above passes through, like any face seen from inside, so the navigation grid, which looks down each column for floors, never mistakes a roof for a floor.

A lit panel is its own breakable glass part (LightPrefix). Shots and blasts break it and its light goes out, leaving the dark housing. Rays only hit its underside and sides, and nothing else collides with panels, so they cost the physics nothing. Each light only lights the inside of its own room (BoxMin to BoxMax), so even without shadows it never shines through a wall. Deterministic from the level's seed, so co-op peers build the same thing.
