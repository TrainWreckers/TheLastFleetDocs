Parameters

overlay ReadOnlySpan<OverlayVertex>

2D triangles drawn over the scene, at most MaxOverlayVertices.

effects ReadOnlySpan<EffectVertex>

World-space effect triangles, at most MaxEffectVertices.

partTints ReadOnlySpan<Vector4>

One tint per world-mesh part (see Parts). rgb is blended over the part by alpha. A negative alpha hides the part: Gone for a part that no longer exists (a broken prop, an opened door), which rays also pass through, and Unseen for a part that's just not drawn this frame (a room out of sight), which rays still hit. If the span is empty or shorter than the part list, the remaining parts aren't tinted.

ui ImDrawDataPtr

Dear ImGui's draw data for this frame (ImGui.GetDrawData() after ImGui.Render()), or default for none.

dynamicWorld ReadOnlySpan<MeshVertex>

World-space, flat-shaded triangles rebuilt every frame (characters, loot, props, hoses), lit like the level. At most MaxDynamicVertices vertices, three per triangle.

viewModel ReadOnlySpan<MeshVertex>

The weapon in your hands: world-space, flat-shaded triangles lit like the level, but drawn with their own projection (ViewModelFieldOfView) and always in front of the world. That way the gun doesn't stretch with the field of view or clip through a wall you're standing against. At most MaxViewModelVertices vertices.

lights ReadOnlySpan<PointLight>

Point lights for the level and characters (not the view model).

shadows ReadOnlySpan<ShadowView>

Shadow maps to draw for some of the lights.

shadowRanges ReadOnlySpan<IndexRange>

The level index ranges the shadow maps draw.

swarm ReadOnlySpan<SwarmInstance>

The horde: one animated body (SetSwarmModel(AnimatedModel, ReadOnlySpan<Vector3>)) drawn at each instance. The first swarmNear use the full body and the rest use the far one. At most MaxSwarmInstances.

swarmNear int

How many of swarm, from the start, are drawn with the full body.

skinned ReadOnlySpan<SkinnedDraw>

Skinned bodies (soldiers and players on a rig). Each one is a mesh (AddSkinnedMesh(ReadOnlySpan<SkinnedVertex>)) skinned by its range of skinMatrices, lit like the level and casting shadows.

skinMatrices ReadOnlySpan<Matrix4x4>

Every skinned body's joint matrices this frame (each combines the joint's inverse bind, its pose and the body's world placement: Skin(in Matrix4x4, Span<Matrix4x4>)), at most MaxSkinMatrices.

viewModelInWorld bool

Whether viewModel is drawn with the world's projection instead of its own. This is used when the viewer's own body holds it (a Near draw), so the hands and the gun line up.

rooms RoomLighting

The level's rooms and their bounce light this frame (RoomLighting). Null for a level with no rooms, where the fill light comes from the sky only.

captures ReadOnlySpan<ReflectionCapture>

Rooms in rooms whose reflections need recapturing (ReflectionCapture). That's every room when a level starts, then only rooms whose lights have changed.

captureLights ReadOnlySpan<PointLight>

The lights for captures, a range per capture.

captureRanges ReadOnlySpan<IndexRange>

The level index ranges captures draw, a range per capture.

fogVolumes ReadOnlySpan<FogVolume>

Smoke in the air this frame (blasts, breaches), at most MaxFogVolumes. The atmosphere's froxel grid adds it to the level's haze.

debris ReadOnlySpan<MeshVertex>

Tumbling pieces of broken props: world-space, flat-shaded triangles like dynamicWorld, drawn and casting shadow map shadows the same way. They're kept out of the ray tracing scene, so a burst of them doesn't mean rebuilding a ray structure every frame. They have a budget of their own (MaxDebrisVertices), so a full dynamicWorld never leaves them out.
