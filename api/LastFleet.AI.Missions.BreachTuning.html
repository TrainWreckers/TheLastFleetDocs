How windows onto space break and what a breach does (content/defs/breaches.json, docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10), the same for every mission: how much a window takes before it's blown out and when its cracks show, how hard and how far the air rushing out pulls, what it does to anyone beside it and what it drags out, and how long the compartment's doors take to seal, the shutter to close and the compartment to repressurize; and how it looks (the shutter's drop, the mist streaming out, the lamps over sealed doors). Whether a breach is for good is the mission recipe's (its breaches block).

How windows onto space break and what a breach does (content/defs/breaches.json, docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10), the same for every mission: how much a window takes before it's blown out and when its cracks show, how hard and how far the air rushing out pulls, what it does to anyone beside it and what it drags out, and how long the compartment's doors take to seal, the shutter to close and the compartment to repressurize; and how it looks (the shutter's drop, the mist streaming out, the lamps over sealed doors). Whether a breach is for good is the mission recipe's (its breaches block).

public BreachTuning(float Toughness, float FirstCracks, float SecondCracks, float PullStrength, float PullReach, float CoreRadius, float CoreDamage, float RemoveWithin, float SealSeconds, float ShutterSeconds, float RepressurizeSeconds, float ShutterDropSeconds = 1.6, float MistPerSecond = 90, float LampFlashes = 1.5)

Parameters

Toughness float

How much damage a window takes before it's blown out (a grenade's blast does up to about 150).

FirstCracks float

The share of its toughness taken when its first cracks show, 0 to 1.

SecondCracks float

The share taken when its cracks spread, 0 to 1, after the first.

PullStrength float

How fast the air drags someone at the breach itself, meters per second (running is 5, sprinting 9).

PullReach float

How far from the breach it pulls, meters.

CoreRadius float

How near the breach someone is caught in it and hurt, meters.

CoreDamage float

How much anyone caught in it is hurt each second.

RemoveWithin float

How near the breach a dropped weapon or item is pulled out through it, meters.

SealSeconds float

How long after the breach its compartment's doors seal, seconds.

ShutterSeconds float

How long after the breach its shutter closes over it (if it's not for good), seconds.

RepressurizeSeconds float

How long after the shutter closes the compartment's doors unseal, seconds.

ShutterDropSeconds float

How long the shutter takes to slide down over the window, seconds (drawn only).

MistPerSecond float

How many motes of mist and frost stream out through a breach each second, while it vents.

LampFlashes float

How many times a second the lamp over a sealed door flashes.
