Remarks

Priorities, highest first: a mantle, an active dash, starting a dash, jumping, then ground or air movement.

Jumps are edge-triggered, so holding Space doesn't jump again on landing. That removes bunny-hopping. In the air, input steers the horizontal velocity toward where the player is pressing, like in Destiny. That works in any direction, whether forward is held or not, at a rate set by the air acceleration. Steering never raises horizontal speed above what the character had at the start of the tick (or its run speed), so momentum only comes from slides and dashes, and turning hard away from the direction you're flying costs speed.

It's a pure function of its inputs, so it's deterministic and can be tested without a world.

Remarks

The target speed keeps whatever speed was carried in along the part of the wish that matches the current heading. So pressing forward and to one side curves the jump without slowing it, a pure strafe turns it at run speed, and pressing back brakes it. With no input, momentum is kept.
