Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Doors

Classes

GateDressing

The markings on each side of a gate between two decks (a locked doorway between regions): a bulkhead frame around the doorway (hazard-striped posts and a header in the color of the deck beyond), a sign beside it with that deck's number, and a band of that color across the floor in front of it. That way a gate is recognizable from across the room as the way into another part of the station, and its colors tell you which part.

Openings

Handles doorway openings in the level. Where two modules' openings meet, the passage is the stretch both are open over. Whatever one opening has beyond the other (for example a wide hall's end against a kit room's 2 m doorway) is walled up in that module's own wall. An unused doorway is walled up all the way across.

StationDoors

How the station's doors are built and how they look. Each is a dark slab filling the doorway, with both faces plated as two leaves in the paint for the door's kind (Plates(in DoorLayout, int, List<DoorPlate>)). A lamp above it is red (amber for the lockdown) and turns green once it's open, and a gate's post gets a keycard reader or release panel. The client slides the same plates apart as it opens. The top of the slab above the leaves stays as a transom, like a doorway's lintel.

Structs

BandCrossing

Where a smooth hall (HallBand: a ring's bend, a bridge on the slant) goes through a doorway between two of its stretches, as a bulkhead crosses it: the stretch of the doorway's grid line inside the hall, which is wider than a doorway where the hall runs on a slant, and the hall's middle and direction there. A door there stands square across the hall, from wall to wall (Door(string, DoorKind, Vector3)), not along the grid.

BulkheadDoor

A door through a bulkhead, between two compartments (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 8): a pressure door where a corridor crosses it. It stands open until one of its compartments seals, when it shuts until both are unsealed. (A service way's hatch through a bulkhead stays open: it's the way round.) A gate (a locked door, LockedDoor) on a bulkhead seals the same way: its part is PartName too.

DoorLayout

A door in a doorway. While shut it's a solid slab the full width, height and thickness of the doorway (its part, PartName). Once it opens, its two leaves slide apart into the walls (drawn by the client, StationDoors).

DoorPlate

A piece of a door's face: a plate on one of its leaves, from From to To along the door from its middle, Low to High up from the floor, standing Proud off the slab.

KeyCard

A keycard lying in the level, opening the lock with the same id.

LockedDoor

A locked door: a level part (PartName) that fills a doorway until it opens. It opens with its keycard, or when the hack finishes for the gate into the last zone (OpensOnHack), or, for a one-way shortcut, when someone stands at it on the side it opens from. OpensFrom is that spot: on the floor in front of the door, at half the wall's height.

RoomDoorSpot

A room's own door in a plan's level: on a doorway from a room onto a hall (or into another room) that isn't a gate, a pressure door or a spawn closet's. It's Auto or Interact, as the room's archetype says (RoomDoor), and a breach in the room seals it (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10).
