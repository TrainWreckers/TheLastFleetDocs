Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Modules

Classes

AngledHall

Builds a stretch of ring hall smooth (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 7): its cells are still the grid's, but its walls follow its band (HallBand: round a ring's bend, or straight on a slant) as straight sides, each a box turned to its side, so the hall reads as a curve instead of steps. The rooms either side keep their walls on the grid.

GltfNodes

Reads the node tree of a .gltf or .glb file: names, transforms and custom properties, but not geometry.

HallArc

A hall bent round a center: a ring hall's corridor (HallBand), the band either side of a circle Radius out from Center.

HallBand

The floor plan of a hall built smooth rather than stepped on the grid (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 7): a band HalfWidth either side of a middle line, on the ground (X and Z, in meters). A ring hall's middle line is a circle (HallArc), a bridge's on a slant a straight line (HallLine). Its walls are the band's edges cut into straight sides no longer than MostSide.

HallLine

A straight hall on a slant: a bridge running out from a ring station's core at any angle (HallBand), the band either side of the line from From to To, open at both ends.

HallTheme

A deck theme and how its halls are laid out (ParametricHall): width and length, how often they're two stories tall, how open they are, and the surface style that sets how they look. A mission template lists its themes (HallThemes, "hallThemes" in its file). If it doesn't, Defaults is used.

KitRooms

A biome's kit of rooms (Kit): the archetypes offered to every level in the biome. Each one is built once, seeded from its own id, so the kit is identical in every level and on every machine, just like the hand-made modules it replaced. Each becomes a module with its archetype's id and fills that archetype's roles: the insertion bay, the corridors and stairwell, the spawn closet, the keycard room, the objective and extraction rooms, and the rooms in between.

ModuleLibrary

A biome's room modules (its kit, KitRooms, plus the level's own), sorted by id so the order is stable.

ModuleReader

Turns a room's scene, as written by a built room or hall (ParametricRoom, ParametricHall), into a RoomModule. It also checks the scene against the kit's rules, so a mistake is reported by name when the room is built instead of showing up as a broken level.

ParametricHall

Procedurally built halls for a deck's theme (HallTheme) in its surface style (SurfaceStyle). The theme sets the size and openness: one to three cells wide, two to four long, one or two stories, and open across a wide end so two halls placed end to end read as one. The style sets the look (Hall): ribs, arches, columns or pillars along the hall; a floor of plain plate, a grated channel lit from below, or light strips along the walls and down the middle; and lighting along the bottom or top of the walls or on whatever stands along the hall (the lighting is in ParametricHall.Lights.cs).

ParametricRoom

Procedurally built rooms, as opposed to hand-made ones: a footprint of any size and shape (a rectangle, or an L, a U, or a cross from a shape recipe), doorways on every side, and furniture laid out in one of a few styles (offices, a warehouse, a lab, a plaza), all from a seed. Each one is built the same way a hand-made module would be, from boxes and markers, and read by ModuleReader. That way it follows the kit's rules (whole cells, clear doorways) and gets spawn spots found from its geometry the same way.

RoomFinish

The finishing details of a built room (ParametricRoom) beyond its footprint: its surface style, its furnishing, how much its corners are cut, and whether pillars hold up its ceiling.

RoomModule

A room that levels are laid out from. It's either built from an archetype (ParametricRoom) or a hall, and checked against the kit's rules by ModuleReader. It holds the geometry, the footprint in grid cells, the connectors, the roles it can fill (tags), and markers in module coordinates.

SceneNode

A node in a glTF scene: its name, world transform, whether it has a mesh, and its custom properties.

ShipBay

A hangar's ship entrance (docs/plans/structure-first.md, hangars): its wall on the hull left open the room's full height, but for a post at each end, behind a field that keeps the air in and people from walking out (shots and the view go through it). A dropship comes in through it to its pad, so the lane from it in is kept clear of anything placed (KeepClear). In the room's own frame.

Structs

ModuleConnector

A doorway on a module's footprint, at the middle of cell Cell along Side, on story Level of the module (0 for the ground floor, 1 for one story up). The opening is Width wide and centered Shift along the side from the cell's middle. That covers both a standard 2 m kit doorway and a wide hall's fully open end. Where two openings of different widths meet, the level walls up whatever part doesn't face the other opening (LevelAssembler).

ModuleLight

An extra light that comes with a module, on top of the ceiling panels (for example a glow under a floor grate or along a trim strip), in module coordinates. Like the panel lights, it only lights its own room. If it has half axes it's a rect light: the glowing surface itself, shining toward the side the cross product of its axes points to (PointLight.Rect).
