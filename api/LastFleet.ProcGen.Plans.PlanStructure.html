A plan's structure for a seed, before any room: its envelope, the halls carved into it, the sections bulkheads cut it into (each with its district), the pressure doors and hatches where halls cross bulkheads, and service stubs so every section a service way passes has a hatch onto a corridor. Also what's wrong with it, for Studio and the tests.

Remarks

Built in a fixed order: the envelope, then the corridors (primary and secondary halls, in the plan's order), then the service ways round them, then the sections, the doors and the stubs. The stubs and the bulkheads' jitter draw from the level's circulation stream (Circulation); everything else follows the envelope's draws, in whole cells, so a seed gives the same structure on every machine.
