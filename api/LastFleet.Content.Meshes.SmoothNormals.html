Remarks

Each corner's normal is the area-weighted average of the face normals of the triangles that meet at that point, in the same group, and that are bent less than CreaseDegrees from its own face. So a barrel's sides blend into one round highlight, while the muzzle rim stays a hard edge.

Fields

CreaseDegrees

Faces angled more than this from each other, in degrees, keep a hard edge between them.
