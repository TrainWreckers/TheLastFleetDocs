The horde (design doc, "Horde simulation"): the Choir's swarmlings, hundreds at once. Not characters: no controller, no physics body, no brain. Each runs over the navigation grid down one shared HordeField towards the nearest player, straight at them once close and in the open, keeps apart from the others by a spatial hash, and lunges: a short wind-up (the telegraph) and a bite. Shots and grenades kill them (the shot and grenade systems test Swarmling bodies) and knock them back; the dead lie a moment and are gone.

Remarks

Hordes come when the mission calls for them: now and then while the squad infiltrates and the Director is building up, with every wave of the hack, and when the extraction starts, with a trickle in between those two. They come out of the floor where the players are not looking, 25 to 55 m away by the way there; or through the station's hull breaches (Breach): a fresh horde may blow one open, 18 to 55 m away by the way and with nobody right by it (in a blast of debris and smoke), and pour in through it, dropping out of the hole into the room; once open, a breach is a way in for every horde after. Call(int) sends one at any time (the developer tools, --horde).

One of the world's late systems (SimulationSetup.CreateWorld's lateSystems): it moves after the characters and logs its bites as hits. Deterministic; allocation-free on most ticks.
