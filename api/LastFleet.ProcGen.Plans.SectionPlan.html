Parameters

Cuts SectionCuts

The bulkheads across a part; null when each part is its own section.

Districts IReadOnlyList<string>

Each section's district (command, crew, cargo...), in order: bow to stern for cuts, one per part (by index) when each part is its own section, or one per painted area. A section without one has none.

Splits IReadOnlyList<RingSplit>

Ring parts cut into sections by angle when each part is its own section.

Paint IReadOnlyList<DistrictPaint>

The painted areas when districts are painted, each its own section; null otherwise. A cell no area paints takes the area of the nearest painted cell, so however the envelope comes out for a seed, every cell has one, and the bulkheads fall where two areas meet.
