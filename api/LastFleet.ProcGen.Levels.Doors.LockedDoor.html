A locked door: a level part (PartName) that fills a doorway until it opens. It opens with its keycard, or when the hack finishes for the gate into the last zone (OpensOnHack), or, for a one-way shortcut, when someone stands at it on the side it opens from. OpensFrom is that spot: on the floor in front of the door, at half the wall's height.
