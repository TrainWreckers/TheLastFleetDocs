Class Rig

Namespace
LastFleet.Content.Rigs
Assembly
LastFleet.Content.dll

A rig: the skeleton bodies of one kind are modeled and animated on (content/rigs/<id>.json; the humanoid first): its bones in their rest pose, the sockets the game uses, the chains IK solves, the masks layers of animation cover, the simulation's numbers the body is fitted to, and the clips made for it. A rig is data, so a four- or eight-legged body is a file, not code. The Blender add-on (tools/blender/lastfleet_rigs.py) builds the rig from the same file and checks models and their animations against it.
