TryFit(ReadOnlySpan<MeshVertex>, ReadOnlySpan<uint>, out Vector3, out Vector3, out Quaternion)

Tries to fit a box: every face normal must lie along one of three perpendicular axes, and every vertex must sit on one of the eight corners. On success, outputs the center, the half extents along those axes, and the rotation from an axis-aligned box to them.

Remarks

The corner rule rejects several boxes joined into one object. Otherwise they'd pass as their combined bounding box, giving a solid block the size of the whole group.
