A vertex of a skinned model in its rest (bind) pose, laid out the way the GPU reads it. It holds the position, the face normal (faces are flat), the color, the four joints that move it with their weights, and its surface data.

A vertex of a skinned model in its rest (bind) pose, laid out the way the GPU reads it. It holds the position, the face normal (faces are flat), the color, the four joints that move it with their weights, and its surface data.

public SkinnedVertex(Vector3 Position, Vector3 Normal, Vector3 Color, uint Joints, Vector4 Weights, uint Surface = 0)

Parameters

Position Vector3

Position in the bind pose, in meters.

Normal Vector3

The face normal in the bind pose.

Color Vector3

The material color (values above 1 glow).

Joints uint

The joint indices into the skeleton, one byte each, with the first in the lowest byte.

Weights Vector4

How much each joint moves the vertex. They add up to 1.

Surface uint

The library material and, for a smooth material, the smoothed normal in the bind pose (VertexSurface).
