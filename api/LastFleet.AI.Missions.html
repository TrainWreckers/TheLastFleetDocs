Namespace LastFleet.AI.Missions

Classes

BattleStations

A boarded warship's crew at battle stations (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"; boarding.crew in campaign.json): the ship knows it's been boarded the moment the pod clamps on, so it never drops below high alert, every soldier is on the move between rooms rather than standing at a post, hunters are sent sooner and given no breathers, and they push the squad harder once they find it.

BoardingPlan

A boarding action's warship (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"): where its gun batteries, its reactor and its fire suppression controls are, how the fight outside goes (how often the fleet's guns hit it, what a hit does, how fires spread and are put out, how hard its batteries hit the fleet), and how its crew fights.

BreachPlan

A hull breach (HullBreaches): the plate that covers it, the plate's bounds, and which direction points into the room.

BreachTuning

How windows onto space break and what a breach does (content/defs/breaches.json, docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10), the same for every mission: how much a window takes before it's blown out and when its cracks show, how hard and how far the air rushing out pulls, what it does to anyone beside it and what it drags out, and how long the compartment's doors take to seal, the shutter to close and the compartment to repressurize; and how it looks (the shutter's drop, the mist streaming out, the lamps over sealed doors). Whether a breach is for good is the mission recipe's (its breaches block).

CargoPodSystem

Runs cargo pod defenses (CargoPod). Once the drone is called, it takes longer to arrive the deeper into the station the squad is (DefenseSeconds(in MissionState)), and the station comes for the pod.

ClosetPlan

A spawn closet: its door part and bounds, the spots where soldiers spawn inside, and the closet's interior bounds.

MissionPlan

A level's mission layout: keycards and locks, the terminal, the extraction point, spawn closets, fallback points where reinforcements can enter when no closet works, hull breaches the horde can come through, and fuel and water tanks to pump into the docked dropship.

MissionSystem

A boarding action (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"): the warship the squad is on is in a fight with the fleet. The fleet's guns hit it on a cadence (each hit counted in HitCount, so everyone aboard feels it); a hit hurts those near it, and sometimes starts a fire or blows a window. Fires spread room to room and burn out, unless the ship's fire suppression is running: its sprinklers put them out, until a hit knocks it out (repaired at its controls in damage control) or its power goes (cutting the reactor kills it). While its batteries fire, the fleet takes damage. The squad silences the batteries and cuts the reactor by holding Interact at each, then takes the bridge; a battery or the reactor lost with its room to a breach is out for good.

MissionTuning

Mission tuning values. Distances are in meters, times in seconds.

MissionWalk

Whether the mission can still be finished with some compartments, or rooms, lost (docs/plans/structure-first.md, decision 8): from the insertion, through doorways whose rooms are both kept, each gate passable once its keycard's room has been reached and the way out once the objective has, can the objective and then an extraction be reached? A breach seals its compartment for good only when this holds without it and every compartment already lost, so two losses can never together lock the squad out, and keycards stay reachable whatever is sealed.

ObjectivePlan

What a mission's squad comes for and how long it takes (docs/plans/structure-first.md, "Objective kinds"): the hack, a download whose drive is carried out, or a self-destruct counted down while the squad gets off.

PatrolRoomPlan

A room the Director's patrols can come out of when no spawn closet will do (a security office, crew quarters): only while nobody is in it, near its spots or can see one, its zone open and its compartment unsealed.

RoomDoorTuning

How rooms' own doors behave (content/defs/doors.json, docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10), the same for every mission: how near someone comes for a sliding door to open and how long it waits to shut behind them; how near a player reaches a door opened by hand, and how long a soldier takes to open one on his way; and how much battering a door opened by hand takes from the swarm before it's broken open for good.

TankPlan

A fuel or water tank in the mission (SupplyTank), with its starting state.

TankPumpSystem

Pumps supply tanks (SupplyTank) into the docked dropship. Whatever gets pumped belongs to the fleet immediately (Delivered), even if the mission fails afterward.

ThreatPlan

How hard a level's places are defended (docs/plans/structure-first.md, "Threat drives the fight"), and the patrols that sets. While the squad is in a room, its threat sets how often the Director sends a patrol and how big, the station's alert speeding both on top.

Structs

BulkheadPlan

A door through a bulkhead that seals (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 8): the level part that fills its doorway, its bounds, and the compartments either side of it.

EscapePodPlan

An escape pod: the level part (surface) of its hatch and the hatch's bounds, the middle of the pod inside, and how many it seats.

FirePanel

A boarded warship's fire suppression panel (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"): holding Interact at it floods its room while it burns, putting the fire out.

MissionKeyPlan

A keycard placed in the level. It opens the lock with the same id.

MissionLockPlan

A locked door: the level part (surface) that fills its doorway, plus its bounds.

ReinforcementPoint

A spawn point for reinforcements, with a yaw that faces into the level.

RoomDoorPlan

A room's own door in a plan's level, as the mission runs it (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10): its part, the rooms either side, and whether it's opened by hand or slides open for whoever comes near.

SuppressionControls

A boarded warship's fire suppression controls (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"), in its damage control: while the system runs, the ship's sprinklers put its fires out; a hit can knock it out, and holding Interact here repairs it.

WindowPlan

A window onto space, as the mission's breaches need it (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10): its parts by surface, where its pane is, the compartment it lets space into, and whether that may ever be lost.
