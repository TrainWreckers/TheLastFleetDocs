The hub between missions: the hangar deck of the fleet's ship, open to space along one side behind its shield. The squad's dropship sits on its pad. Along the walls are the mission console (pick and launch the next mission), the needs board (what the fleet has and what it's short of), the armory bench (weapons, parts, the pack) and the star map table. It's a hand-built level made in Blender (content/modules/hub/hangar_deck.gltf, docs/blender/hub.md), so it's the same on every machine.

Remarks

Load(string, HubStations?) checks the file's rules: every object that collides must be a box (an object named ...NoCollision is only drawn); an empty named Spawn_Player marks where the squad comes in, facing its local -Z; and an empty named Station<name> for each HubStation marks the spot on the deck where that station is used from.
