A set piece a designer pinned to a plan (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 11): a room of an archetype that goes here, or where the squad boards. Like a stair core (PlanCore), it's placed on a part, so it follows the part however long or wide a seed draws it: along a line (a hull, a box, an arm), as a share of its length and cells off its middle line; round a ring, as cells from its center at its fewest radius, grown with it.
