Classes

Decks

A station's decks. Each deck is a zone behind a keycard gate (a room's region). Gives each deck's name and color, which its room baseboards, gate bulkheads and signs, and the HUD use, so you always know which deck you're on and which one you're heading into.

LevelAssembler

Pass 3 (design doc): turns the placed modules into one level mesh (with unused doorways walled up and locked doors in place) and populates it. That means player spawns from the insertion, soldier squads from the rooms' spawn points within the template's soldier budget, weapons, the objective and extraction. After that come the props the placement rules put in the rooms (LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationPlacements): keycards, ammo crates, containers, cargo, supplies and tanks.

MissionLayout

The mission's key points in a generated level: where the team arrives, the keys and locks, the objective, the extraction point, the spawn closets, and fallback reinforcement spawns (just inside the arena's doorways, facing in) for layouts with no closet by the arena. Also the weapons and supplies lying around, the containers to search, the cargo to carry out, the tanks to pump into the dropship, and the hull breaches the horde can come through.

PatrolRoom

A room patrols can come out of when no spawn closet will do (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 14): one the recipe's threat names a source (a security office, crew quarters), with the spots soldiers start from in it.

RoomRects

Splits a placed room's ground story into rectangles of whole cells that cover it exactly once. A rectangular room is just its own bounds; an L becomes two. Anything laid out on a grid across a room (ceiling panels, the ceiling body) is laid out on each rectangle.

Structs

CornerChamfer

A room corner cut at 45° (Chamfer). It applies to a corner of the outline where the room is on one side only and neither of the two walls meeting there has a doorway. The cut runs Size meters along each wall from where their inner faces meet. It's built as a solid wedge in the corner: a box rotated 45°, since that's the only solid level collision supports besides an axis-aligned box. Its face is the cut and the rest sits inside the walls. Anything that runs along the walls (trims, a duct, lockers) stops short of it (WallRun).

PartRooms

The room a level part belongs to, or the two rooms a doorway's part (a lintel, a locked door) sits between, so the client can skip drawing parts of rooms it cannot see. -1 for none: such a part is always drawn.

WallRun

A straight stretch of a placed room's walls on its ground story, used by the dressing to run things along it (trims, ducts, pipes). Holds which way it faces out of the room, where it runs (in meters, along X for a north or south wall, along Z for an east or west one), where its inner face is on the cross axis, and which way is into the room (+1 or -1).
