Procedurally built halls for a deck's theme (HallTheme) in its surface style (SurfaceStyle). The theme sets the size and openness: one to three cells wide, two to four long, one or two stories, and open across a wide end so two halls placed end to end read as one. The style sets the look (Hall): ribs, arches, columns or pillars along the hall; a floor of plain plate, a grated channel lit from below, or light strips along the walls and down the middle; and lighting along the bottom or top of the walls or on whatever stands along the hall (the lighting is in ParametricHall.Lights.cs).

Remarks

To the layout these are corridors, offered on their own deck alongside the kit's narrow corridors. They're read by ModuleReader like any other module, so they follow the kit's rules (whole cells, clear doorways).

Anything hanging under the ceiling (a frame's beam, an arch's haunches) has no top face for rays, the same as the station's lintels, so the navigation grid never mistakes it for floor. It gets its own collision body to stop characters. Floor lights are render-only and flush with the floor. Generation is deterministic: the same seed, theme and style always give the same hall.
