What the path tracer does this frame, from its settings and what DLSS can do here: the size it renders at, which denoiser runs, whether DLSS upscales (Super Resolution) or denoises and upscales (Ray Reconstruction), and whether the camera is jittered for it. Worked out every frame, so a setting changed in the menu applies on the next one.
