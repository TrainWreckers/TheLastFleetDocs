A network as laid in a level, for designers to inspect (the Studio preview): the route it took, what each section runs along, its cost, and the cheapest route it didn't take (Alternative(LaneGraph, NetworkSpec, NetworkRoute, IReadOnlyList<int>, LaneUsage?)). The alternative is only computed the first time it's asked for.
