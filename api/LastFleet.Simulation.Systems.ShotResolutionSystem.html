Remarks

Characters are hit through HitVolumes (a body capsule and a head sphere), tested with exact math, never through the physics engine. A shot stops at the first thing it hits. A character on the shooter's own side stops it without taking damage.

A round is one hit: health drops pellet by pellet, but the flinch, the stagger and the push (ImpactRules) come once a round for each body it hit, from all its pellets on that body added up.

Runs right after the weapon system, so shots start from where the shooter stood when firing. With lagCompensation, a co-op client's shots hit other characters where that client saw them.
