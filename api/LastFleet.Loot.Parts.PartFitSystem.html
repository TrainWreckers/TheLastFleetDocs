Handles fitting weapon parts, anywhere and at any time (FitPart). A part from the pack goes onto one of the two weapons, replacing the part in that slot, and the replaced part goes into the pack in its place.

Remarks

Fitting takes the part's fit time (its item's fitSeconds) with the weapon lowered, and getting hit cancels it (the part stays in the pack). The weapon is rebuilt from its roll with the new part (Roll(WeaponIdentity)), so its stats, model and card update. The magazine and reserve keep what they hold, capped at the new sizes.

This is a command system, because it needs the loot data, which the simulation doesn't have. It runs at the start of a tick, before the inventory system, which leaves an in-progress part fitting to it. Only rolled weapons take parts, and relics don't. It doesn't allocate except on the tick a part goes on.
