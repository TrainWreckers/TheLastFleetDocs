Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Plans

Classes

ArmPart

A straight run of cells out from a point: a station's arm, a ring station's bridge, a docking boom. It grows from its At in its direction for a length picked from its range, as wide as its width either side of its line, so it stays on its line however long it's drawn.

BoxModule

A rectangle of cells: a station's hub or arm, a ship's sponson, a module bolted on. Its size is picked from its ranges; it can be mirrored across the centerline (a pair of sponsons from one part).

BridgePart

A straight run out from a center at any angle: a ring station's bridge from its core to its ring, on a slant as often as not (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 7). It covers every cell its band (Width cells across) touches, from Span's first count of cells out from the center's middle to its second, so a hall laid on it runs smooth along its line (HallLine) rather than stepped on the grid.

CrossHall

A hall straight across a part, at a share of its length: a cross passage joining a ship's passageways, a hub's corridor across it. It reaches Reach cells either side of the part's line, or as far as the envelope allows with Inset cells to spare.

DistrictPaint

Cells a designer painted with one of a plan's districts (Paint), as rows of text the way a mask is (PaintedMask): # for a painted cell, anything else for none. The first row is the one with the least Z, and each row's first character the cell with the least X.

DistrictProgram

What a district is made of (districts.json): its anchor, the set piece its section is built round (a mess, a cargo hold, the bridge), and the rooms that fill the rest of its plots.

DistrictRoom

A kind of room a district is made of (DistrictProgram): the archetype it's built from (its style, furnishing and tags; its shape comes from the plot it's cut from), how many cells along its hall it takes, how often it's picked, whether it comes in a row of the same, and whether it's somewhere you go into rather than through.

Envelope

The outline of a level on one deck: the grid cells inside its hull, built from a plan's parts for a seed. Everything later in the structure-first pipeline (halls, sections, rooms) happens inside it, and its hull edges are where the level meets space.

ExitPlanner

Finds a plan level's ways on and off (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 4): airlock vestibules, small rooms on the hull that open only onto a corridor, so the squad boards into a hallway rather than a store; and rooms on the hull for the squad to be taken off from.

FillHall

A hall that is the whole of the part it's laid on: every cell of it inside the envelope, whatever its shape. A mask painted as a hallway, a box made a concourse, an arm that is all corridor.

HallPart

One of a plan's halls, carved after the envelope and before any room. A hall is laid on one of the plan's parts (On) and follows the shape that part was drawn at (PartShape), so it fits however long or wide the part came out. Halls are carved in order; corridors first, then service ways round them.

HullProfile

A ship's hull: a length and a beam picked from their ranges, and a profile saying how wide it is along its length. It's mirrored across its centerline, with the bow at its At and the stern toward +X.

LevelPlan

A kind of level, as a designer composed it in Studio from parts: a frigate, a carrier, a station of hub and spokes, a ring station. Its envelope is built from its parts in order (Build(LevelPlan, ulong, int)), each adding cells or cutting them out. Its halls are carved into the envelope next, then it's cut into sections (Build(LevelPlan, ulong, int)).

MissionEmbedder

Reads a mission into a plan's level before its rooms are built (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 4), by the template's recipe (PlanRecipe). The structure is already there, so nothing is grown: the embedder picks among the rooms the level has.

PaintedMask

Cells a designer painted, as rows of text: # for a cell, anything else for none. The first row is the one with the least Z, and each row's first character the cell with the least X.

PlanCore

A plan's stair core: stairs from one deck up to the one above, at a fixed frame of the ship, so it's in the same place on every seed. It stands as near the frame as a stairwell fits, clear of the halls on both decks, its foot joined to a corridor on the lower deck and its landing to one on the upper by short passages where they don't touch.

PlanDeck

One deck of a plan (Decks): its name, and its sections' districts if not the plan's.

PlanLevel

A plan's level for a seed, built from the outside in: its structure (PlanStructure), then the space its halls leave cut into rooms from each section's district program, then every room's doors, then every piece built as a room module and placed on the grid.

PlanMission

The mission read into a plan's level (MissionEmbedder): each piece's zone and role, each doorway's lock, the way through, and the mission graph the rest of generation reads, in which each piece is the node of its own index.

PlanMissionException

A mission recipe can't be read into a plan's level for a seed: no room on the hull for an airlock, or too few sections for two zones. The generator tries a seed made from this one.

PlanPart

One part of a level's plan (LevelPlan). Each part covers some grid cells, picked from its own random stream within the ranges a designer gave, and adds them to the envelope or cuts them out of it.

PlanPicture

Draws a plan's structure from above as a picture, for checking a plan by eye outside Studio (Studio's --plan-png): its sections tinted, its halls (main corridors palest, service ways brown), its bulkheads in red, its pressure doors in yellow and hatches in orange, the hull's edges, the centerline, and a mark every five cells (20 m). The bow is to the left.

PlanPiece

One piece of a plan's level, built as one room module: a room cut from a plot, or a stretch of hall. Long halls are built in stretches at most 8 cells each way, joined by openings as wide as the hall.

PlanPin

A set piece a designer pinned to a plan (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 11): a room of an archetype that goes here, or where the squad boards. Like a stair core (PlanCore), it's placed on a part, so it follows the part however long or wide a seed draws it: along a line (a hull, a box, an arm), as a share of its length and cells off its middle line; round a ring, as cells from its center at its fewest radius, grown with it.

PlanStructure

A plan's structure for a seed, before any room: its envelope, the halls carved into it, the sections bulkheads cut it into (each with its district), the pressure doors and hatches where halls cross bulkheads, and service stubs so every section a service way passes has a hatch onto a corridor. Also what's wrong with it, for Studio and the tests.

RingHall

A hall round a ring part: a ring station's ring corridor, the corridor round its core. It runs FromOutside cells in from the ring's outside, Across meters wide, walkable all the way round, built smooth round its bend (AngledHall) on every cell it touches.

RingPart

A ring of cells round a center: a ring station's outer ring, or a disc when it's as wide as its radius (a hub). A cell is in it when its middle is within the outer radius and beyond the inner one, worked out in whole numbers.

RingSplit

A ring part cut into sections by angle, when each part is its own section (ByParts): a bulkhead out from its center at each of Quarters, so a ring station's ring is several sections, each its own zone, rather than one. The first piece round from the first quarter keeps the part's own section; the others get sections of their own after every part's, with the part's district.

SectionCuts

Bulkheads across one of a plan's parts, at shares of its length: a ship cut bow to stern into sections. Every cell of the envelope takes the section of where it falls along that part's line.

SectionPlan

How a plan's envelope is cut into sections (the parts between bulkheads, which become zones), and what each one is: bulkheads across one part (Cuts), each part its own section (a station's hub, each arm, each bridge), or districts painted on the plan's cells (Paint).

SpineHall

A hall along a part's line: a ship's spine, a passageway either side of it, a station arm's corridor, a bridge. It runs from From to To of the part's length (shares that may go past 0 and 1 to reach into the parts beside it), at Offset cells across, and stops where the envelope ends or leaves fewer than Inset cells either side of it for rooms. Only its longest stretch is kept, so it never breaks in two.

Structs

ExitPoint

A way on or off a plan's level: a room against the hull and the side of it the hull is on, or a hangar the dropship lands in.

PartShape

The shape a part was drawn at for a seed, so halls and sections can follow it however long or wide it came out: a line it runs along (from its origin, in its direction, for its length, with cells either side of it), or a ring's center, radius and width.

PlanDock

Where the dropship docks to take the squad off: one side of a cell of the extraction room, on the hull with no doorway in it, where the airlock's outer hatch goes.

PlanDoor

A door in a plan's structure, on one side of a cell.

PlanDoorway

A doorway between two pieces of a plan's level, on one side of a cell of the first.

ProfilePoint

A point on a hull's profile (HullProfile): how wide the hull is at a place along it.

Enums

HallTier

What a hall is for, which says how wide it reads and what it joins.

PartMode

What a plan's part does to the envelope built so far.

PlanDoorKind

The doors a plan's structure puts in before any room: where halls cross bulkheads.

PlanDoorwayKind

What joins two pieces of a plan's level.

PlanPieceKind

What a piece of a plan's level is: a room, a stretch of corridor or of service way, a spawn closet or stairs.

PlanPinKind

What a plan's pin holds in place (PlanPin).
