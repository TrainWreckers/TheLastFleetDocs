Remarks

A part's origin is its attach point. Where the object sits in the file is ignored, so parts can be laid out side by side while working on them. Its rotation and scale are applied, though. Meshes below it are part of it, and empties below it are its markers.

Triangles keep their material name, so the game can color some materials itself (a manufacturer's finish, a rarity color). They also keep their library material, with smoothed normals for smooth materials (SmoothNormals, within the part). Textures, skins and animation are ignored. Blender's duplicate suffixes (.001) are stripped from every name, so two parts can each have a "Muzzle".
