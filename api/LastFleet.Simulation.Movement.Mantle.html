Climbing onto ledges, Witchfire style. If you jump at a ledge while pressing forward, the character pulls itself up and over with the weapon lowered, instead of bumping into it.

Remarks

A ledge is a wall at knee height in front of the character, with a flat top between MantleMinHeight and MantleMaxHeight above the feet and room to stand on it. Lower obstacles are steps the body just walks up. Taller ones need a double jump first.
