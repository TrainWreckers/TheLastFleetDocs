Remarks

Each player's Stress goes up when they take damage or get downed, and fades once they're safe. The squad's stress is that of the most stressed player still standing. During build-up, a squad left alone too long gets a patrol from the nearest spawn closet (never one in view, near anyone, or behind a gate it hasn't opened), sized for the number of players. At a peak, nothing new gets sent. While relaxing, the hack's waves are delayed a little (HoldWaves).

On top of that, the station's alert (Alert) rises with time on the level and with noise, and barely drops. The higher the alert, the sooner, bigger and more numerous the patrols (AlertTuning). Like in Alien: Isolation, the Director knows where the squad is but its soldiers don't: it sends them to look nearby, sweeping room to room, and pulls them back if they crowd the squad too long without a fight (Hunt(ref DirectorState, float, bool, float)).

Runs with the command systems, after the MissionSystem, whose closets it uses. Only a mission level has a DirectorState (Spawn(GameWorld)); without one this does nothing. Deterministic, and doesn't allocate except on ticks where it adds a player's stress component or sends a patrol.
