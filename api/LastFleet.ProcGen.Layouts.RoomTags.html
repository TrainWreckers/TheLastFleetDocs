Works out the tags the generator gives each placed room (BuiltInTags): what the room is for (role.) and where it is in the layout. That covers its deck (deck.), whether it's one of the kit's own mission rooms (type.kit), and whether it's on the mission's main path, a dead end, or on a loop. A built room also gets the content tags its archetype gave it, and a boarding action's rooms what the warship has there (mission.battery, mission.reactor, mission.suppression).

Remarks

Spawn closets only get their role tag, and they're left out of the layout's graph, so a closet never makes a room count as a dead end or as being on a loop.
