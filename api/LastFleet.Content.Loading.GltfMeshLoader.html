Remarks

Walks the file's scene, placing every triangle primitive with its node's transform. Each triangle gets a face normal computed after the transform, which is the grey-box look and makes the file's own normals irrelevant. Each primitive gets its material's color (MaterialColors: base color plus emission, so a lamp can be brighter than white) and its library material (Resolve(string?, string?, bool)). Corners of smooth materials get smoothed normals for their highlights (SmoothNormals). Textures, skins, morph targets and animation are ignored.

Identical vertices are merged, then meshoptimizer reorders triangles for the GPU's vertex cache and vertices for fetch locality.
