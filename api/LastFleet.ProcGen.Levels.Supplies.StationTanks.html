The station's supply tanks (TankSpot). The models for the template's tank kinds (TankKind, LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationProp) are baked into the level mesh where the tank rule places them (LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationPlacements). Each one is no further from the airlock's port, walking distance for the squad, than the template's hose reaches. Each starts as full as the template says (some are empty), and its gauge, at the model's Gauge and Gauge_Top markers, shows how full it is. The walk from the port and the spots against walls are used for the reservoir too.
