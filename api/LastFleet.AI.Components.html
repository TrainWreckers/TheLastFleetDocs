Namespace LastFleet.AI.Components

Structs

ChoirBrain

The host-only AI state of a Choir unit (ChoirUnit is the part everyone sees). Holds its path to its target and what it last found between itself and the target.

CoverRejects

Cover points (indices in the CoverMap) a soldier has ruled out for now: the way there passed too close to the enemy, or there was no way (CoverRejects).

DirectorState

The Director's state. It lives on a single entity, and each mission level has one.

IntentScores

One utility score per SoldierIntent.

PathPoints

A soldier's path, as its corner points in order.

SoldierBrain

Everything a soldier's AI remembers between ticks. It's plain data so it can be inspected and checksummed.

SoldierVariant

A soldier's enemy variant on the client side, as an index into EnemyRoster. It's sent with each soldier so the client can draw the right model. On the host, soldiers keep this in their SoldierBrain instead.

Stress

A player's stress level, read by the Director. It goes up when the player takes damage or gets downed, and fades when they're safe.

TacticalInputs

The inputs a soldier used for its utility scores the last time it picked an intent. Kept for the debugger.

Enums

DirectorPhase

The Director's current phase (design doc, "AI director"), modeled on Left 4 Dead's: build-up, peak, relax.

IntentReason

The consideration that weighed most in the chosen intent. This is the "why" the AI debugger shows.

SoldierIntent

What a soldier fighting from cover plans to do next, picked by utility scoring (see TacticalUtility). The role is the soldier's job in the squad; the intent is how it's playing that job right now.

SoldierState

What a soldier is doing.

SquadRole

A soldier's job in its squad, set by the squad coordinator.
