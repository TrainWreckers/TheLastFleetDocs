Remarks

Zones are runs of sections, numbered out from the insertion's airlock, sections the same number of bulkheads from it sharing a zone. Every way from one zone into the next is a gate. The objective is in the zone before the last, its hack opening the gate into the last, where extraction is. Each other gate's keycard is in the zone before it, off the way through.
