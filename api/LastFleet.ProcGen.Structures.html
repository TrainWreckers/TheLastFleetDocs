Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Structures

Classes

EdgeClassifier

Works out a room's structure from its shape: the floor and ceiling, the walls as straight runs between corners (cut open at the doorways), the doorways, and the corners. Each is a StructureMarker in the room's own coordinates. Surface styles build geometry from them, so no wall ever has to be placed or rotated by hand.

FineGrid

One story of a room at CellSize resolution: which half-meter squares of its floor are inside the room. Shapes finer than the layout's 4 m grid (chamfered corners, insets, pillars) get carved out here, and walls, floors and ceilings are found from its edges.

StructureMarker

A piece of a room's structure, found from the room's shape: what it is, where it is, which way it faces, and how long it is. Surface styles build geometry from markers, and placement rules anchor props to them.

Structs

FineRect

A block of fine cells (FineGrid), starting at (X, Z) and Width by Depth in size.

MarkerFrame

Where a piece of structure is and which way it faces: a point on its surface, the direction into the room (Inward), and the direction along it (Along). Walls get built and props get placed in this frame, so nothing generated ever has to be rotated by hand.

WallOpening

A gap in a room's wall: a doorway, or a hall's end open all across. Given as two points on the room's outline in its own coordinates (meters, X and Z), on one story; the wall runs between them are left open.
