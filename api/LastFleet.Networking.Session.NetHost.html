The host side of co-op. Accepts clients (checking their version), spawns a player for each one, applies their commands to their players every tick, and sends them snapshots of the world and the tick's events.

Remarks

Call Receive() and ApplyInputs() before each simulation step and Publish(in TickEvents) after it. The host's own player is still driven locally.
