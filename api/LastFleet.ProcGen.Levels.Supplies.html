Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Supplies

Classes

StationReservoirs

Deep reservoirs (the template's ReservoirSettings). A reservoir is a big tank in a room further from the airlock's port than a hose reaches, plus an outlet on a wall near the airlock. The two are joined by the reservoir feed (LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Supplies.ReservoirFeed, a network routed along the walls and ceilings), with one valve at the reservoir and some along the way (ValveSpot). In game terms it's a TankSpot at the outlet: players take the hose there once every valve is open, and the gauge there shows the reservoir's level. Reservoirs are placed after everything else in the level (tanks, containers, cargo), using their own random stream, so they don't change anything else a seed produces. The reservoir, outlet and valves use the template's models (LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationProp).

StationTanks

The station's supply tanks (TankSpot). The models for the template's tank kinds (TankKind, LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationProp) are baked into the level mesh where the tank rule places them (LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationPlacements). Each one is no further from the airlock's port, walking distance for the squad, than the template's hose reaches. Each starts as full as the template says (some are empty), and its gauge, at the model's Gauge and Gauge_Top markers, shows how full it is. The walk from the port and the spots against walls are used for the reservoir too.

Structs

CargoSpot

A cargo crate in the level that players carry out to the fleet. Its contents and amount are rolled from Table when the fight is set up. If the station was chosen for what it holds, most crates hold that.

ContainerSpot

A closed container to search. Either a footlocker on the floor (its body is part of the level, so it's solid) or a case on a desk or shelf. The contents are rolled from Table when the fight is set up.

LootSpot

A weapon lying in the level: rolled from loot table Table at item level Level.

SupplySpot

A pile lying in the level, rolled from item table Table (content/defs/loot/items.json) when the fight is set up: a medkit, an ammo pack, a weapon part, a few of a supply...

TankSpot

A storage tank of fuel or water in the level (its model is baked into the level mesh) that the squad empties into the docked dropship. Its hose runs back through the station to the airlock's port and gets pumped while the ship is docked.

ValveSpot

A valve on a reservoir's pipe (Valves): its wheel, and where a player stands to turn it.
