A door in a doorway. While shut it's a solid slab the full width, height and thickness of the doorway (its part, PartName). Once it opens, its two leaves slide apart into the walls (drawn by the client, StationDoors).

A door in a doorway. While shut it's a solid slab the full width, height and thickness of the doorway (its part, PartName). Once it opens, its two leaves slide apart into the walls (drawn by the client, StationDoors).

Parameters

PartName string

Its part: the slab. The leaf faces belong to PartName/Leaves.

Kind DoorKind

What kind of door it is.

Floor Vector3

The middle of the doorway, on the floor.

Along Vector3

The direction across the doorway along the wall, which is the way the leaves slide (unit, level).

Width float

How wide the doorway is, in meters.

Height float

How tall the slab is, in meters: the leaves plus the transom above them up to the top of the wall.

Thickness float

How thick it is, in meters: both walls, or the airlock's single wall.

Sealed Vector3

For a one-way shortcut, the direction out of the doorway toward the side it can't be opened from (unit). Zero for any other door.
