Builds the station's hull breaches (HullBreach): weak spots in the outer hull, a few in walls with nothing behind them and a few in roofs, spread through the station with at most one per room. Each is a patched plate (its own part, Prefix) sitting a few centimeters off the wall or ceiling over a ragged black hole (HolePrefix, render only). When the plate is blown away, the hole shows.

Remarks

Wall breaches only go on the ground story's walls, between the baseboard and the stripe, and never at a doorway. Roof breaches only go where they're clear of the light panels. If something stands in front of one (a container against the wall, a catwalk under a roof), the horde detects that when the level loads and doesn't use it. Deterministic from the level's seed.
