Namespace
LastFleet.ProcGen.Seeding

The stages of a level's generation. Each has its own random streams (Streams).

public enum GenerationStage

Fields

Mission = 0

The mission graph: rooms, roles, keys and locks.

Typing = 1

Picks which room archetype is used for each room in the mission graph.

Layout = 2

Places the rooms' footprints on the grid, with their doorways and loops.

Structure = 3

Works out the walls, floors, ceilings and so on from the rooms' footprints.

Styles = 4

Builds the structure in its surface styles.

Placement = 5

Places props by rule.

Networks = 6

Routes pipes, ducts and conduits over walls and ceilings.

Loot = 7

What there is to find, by room type and zone.

Validation = 8

Checks and repairs.

Envelope = 9

A plan's envelope: the cells inside its hull, one stream per part (structure-first levels).

Circulation = 10

A plan's halls, sections, bulkheads and hatches (structure-first levels).

Plots = 11

A plan's plots cut into rooms, and their doors (structure-first levels).

Embedding = 12

A plan level's exits and mission: zones, gates, keycards, the objective and extraction (structure-first levels).
