Delta compression for snapshots. Each snapshot is sent as a delta from one the client has acknowledged. Its bytes are XORed with the baseline's, so anything unchanged becomes zero, and the result is sent as runs: a count of unchanged bytes, then a count of changed bytes followed by those bytes, and so on.

Remarks

It doesn't know anything about what's in a snapshot apart from its sections. That works because the snapshot writer lays the world out the same way every tick: a soldier standing still costs nothing, and a moving one costs a few bytes of position. A snapshot starts with a table of its section lengths (BeginSections(NetWriter, int)), and each section is compared against the same section of the baseline. So when a list grows or shrinks, only the rest of that list shifts, not everything after it.

Without a baseline (the first snapshot, or after a long stretch of packet loss), the delta from nothing is just the whole snapshot.
