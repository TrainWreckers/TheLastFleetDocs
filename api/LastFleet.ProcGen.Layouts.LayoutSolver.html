Remarks

First a backtracking search puts each room (with up to two corridors before it) at a free connector of its parent, with no overlaps. Then it adds loops between rooms that are far apart in the tree: directly, through a short straight corridor where two doorways face each other, or through a corridor routed around corners across empty cells (at most MaxRouteCells) between any two free doorways on a story. Loops never cross regions, so a loop can't get around a lock.

Next it keeps adding loops until the fraction of rooms on a loop reaches what the recipe asks for (Share). Then it adds one-way shortcuts across the gates between zones, each opening only from the deeper zone's side (OneWayPerGate), so none of them gets around a lock either. A layout with fewer loops than the template asks for is thrown away and tried again.
