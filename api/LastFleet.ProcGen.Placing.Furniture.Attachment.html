Extra furniture placed with a piece at its model's Attach_<Group>_<n> empties (the attach field in props.json), for example chairs around a table or a stool at a counter. Each one is placed with its origin at the empty, facing the piece, either tucked in or pulled out, and rotated a little. It's placed and checked together with the piece.

Parameters

Group string

Which empties to use. Seat means every Attach_Seat_* on the model.

Prop PropDefinition

The furniture to place there. It can't have attachments of its own.

Chance float

The chance (0 to 1) that each empty gets one.

Pulled float

The fraction (0 to 1) that are pulled out from the piece. The rest are tucked in at the empty.

Pull float

How far a pulled-out one sits back from the empty, in meters.

Jitter float

The maximum random rotation either way, in degrees.
