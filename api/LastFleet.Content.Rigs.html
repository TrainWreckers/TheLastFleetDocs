Namespace LastFleet.Content.Rigs

Classes

Rig

A rig: the skeleton bodies of one kind are modeled and animated on (content/rigs/<id>.json; the humanoid first): its bones in their rest pose, the sockets the game uses, the chains IK solves, the masks layers of animation cover, the simulation's numbers the body is fitted to, and the clips made for it. A rig is data, so a four- or eight-legged body is a file, not code. The Blender add-on (tools/blender/lastfleet_rigs.py) builds the rig from the same file and checks models and their animations against it.

RigBody

The simulation's body measurements that a rig's body is fitted to.

RigBone

A bone of a Rig, in its rest pose.

RigChain

A chain of bones that IK solves from root to tip, for example an arm, a leg, or the spine and head.

RigClip

An animation made for a rig, identified by name.

RigMask

The set of bones an animation layer affects, for example the upper body or the arms. Bones are indices in Bones.

RigSocket

A named point on a bone that the game uses, for example where the first-person camera sits or where a hand holds the gun.

Enums

RigChainTarget

What an IK chain reaches for.

RigEye

Where a clip keeps the body's eye. It's either pinned to where the simulation puts it for standing or crouched (so the head is where shots hit and the first-person camera lines up), or free (falling dead, downed, leaning out).

RigTranslation

Where a bone's offset from its parent comes from when a clip plays on a model of the rig (like Unreal's bone translation retargeting). This lets models on the same rig have different builds, such as longer arms or a shorter body, and still share its clips.
