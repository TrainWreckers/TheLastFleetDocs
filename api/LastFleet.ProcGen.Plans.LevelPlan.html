A kind of level, as a designer composed it in Studio from parts: a frigate, a carrier, a station of hub and spokes, a ring station. Its envelope is built from its parts in order (Build(LevelPlan, ulong, int)), each adding cells or cutting them out. Its halls are carved into the envelope next, then it's cut into sections (Build(LevelPlan, ulong, int)).
