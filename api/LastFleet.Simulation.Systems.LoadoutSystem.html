Namespace

Switches weapons. When a command asks for another slot, the weapon in hand is stowed in its slot and the new one is brought up, and it can't fire until its swap time has passed. A reload in progress is lost, like in Destiny.

Take(ref Loadout, ref WeaponStats, ref WeaponState, in WeaponSlot, out WeaponSlot)

Picks up weapon from the world. It goes into a free slot and is brought up right away. If every slot is full, it replaces the weapon in hand, which is returned in dropped (with its ammo) so the caller can leave it where the new one was.
