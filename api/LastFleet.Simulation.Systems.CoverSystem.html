Remarks

Crouched behind low cover (LowMin to LowMax tall), it ducks below the top, and aiming peeks just over it. Moving away from where it took cover leaves cover.

A player leans out to either side anywhere with LeanLeft and LeanRight (Q and E): the eye, the shots and the head move up to LeanDistance to the side, less where a wall is in the way, and the body stays where it is. A player is never slowed by cover and never blind fires.

A soldier facing a wall with an edge beside it stands behind the wall, and aiming leans it out around the edge. Firing without aiming is blind fire: the gun goes over or around the cover, the body stays hidden, and the shots are wild. While peeking a soldier moves slowly.

Runs after movement and mantles (it overrides the eye height they set) and before weapons (which fire from the eye position it leaves). It looks for cover with a few rays every CheckTicks ticks per character, spread out over the ticks, and never while peeking (the cover is kept until the peek ends). It's deterministic, so the client predicts its own cover.
