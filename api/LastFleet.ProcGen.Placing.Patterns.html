Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Patterns

Classes

FurniturePattern

A furniture pattern (a furnishing's patterns in rules.json): a small grid of half-meter cells, drawn as the room is seen from above, saying what has to be where (a wall behind, open floor in front, a desk beside) and what it places there. A room finds every spot and turn it fits, scores them, and keeps the best until it has as many as its count (LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Patterns.PatternPlacer).

PatternFit

Whether a pattern fits at a cell turned one way, and why not (Probe(FurniturePattern, int, int)).

PatternPiece

One piece a FurniturePattern places: its prop, standing in the middle of its block of cells (and pushed back against any wall the block touches), facing the way it says.

PatternPlacement

What a room's furniture patterns placed (LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Patterns.PatternPlacer).

PatternPreview

A built room as its furnishing's patterns left it, for showing a designer what their patterns do: the room, its cells, what the patterns placed, how every pattern and rule did, and why a pattern does or doesn't fit at a cell.

PatternSymbols

The fixed symbols a pattern's cells are drawn with in rules.json. Letters are the pattern's own, given meaning by its key: a piece it places, or a prop that has to be there already.

RoomCells

A room as furniture patterns see it: its floor in half-meter cells (the cells of its LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Reach.ReachGrid, with a ring of one more around them), each a wall, a doorway, floor or the walkway between its doorways, and what stands on it so far.

PatternCell

One cell of a FurniturePattern.

PatternTurn

One way a pattern can lie in a room: turned clockwise by Quarters quarter turns as seen from above (none: its top row to the north), after being mirrored left to right if Mirrored.

PatternFacing

The way one of a pattern's pieces faces, as the pattern is drawn: toward its bottom row, its top row, or either side. The pattern turns and mirrors as it's matched, and its pieces turn with it.

RoomCellKind
