Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Airlocks

Classes

Airlock

Builds the airlock into the insertion bay: a chamber around the player spawns against the bay's wall that has no doorway (the hull), the outer hatch in that wall where the dropship is docked, and an inner wall with a door the mission opens once the lock has cycled (AirlockLayout).

AirlockLayout

The airlock the squad starts in, built into the insertion bay against its outer wall. The dropship is docked at the hatch in that wall, and an inner door (the level part DoorPart) opens once the lock has cycled.

Dropship

The dropship landed in a hangar, for a level boarded by its hangar rather than an airlock (docs/plans/structure- first.md, Phase 4). The squad starts in its hold, behind its rear hatch, which the mission opens once everyone is aboard and the ramp is down, just as an airlock's inner door. Its intake and cargo chute are on its hull, so tanks are hosed to it and cargo loaded into it where it stands. It's built of boxes along the hangar's length, its tail toward the hangar's doors. A mission that leaves by a hangar gets one landed there too, its hatch open, the squad taken off from its hold.
