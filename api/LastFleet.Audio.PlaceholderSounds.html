Remarks

Covers gunshots for each weapon frame, footsteps, impacts, breaking glass, wood and metal, explosions, reloads, hit markers, pickups, mission cues, a light's buzz and the station's hum. Think of it as grey-box sound: good enough to play with, and meant to be replaced.
