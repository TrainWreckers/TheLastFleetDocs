Moves characters through the level: velocity in, position, collision-adjusted velocity and ground contact out. Also swaps the standing and crouching capsules, keeps a character crouched where it has no room to stand, and keeps characters from walking through each other.

Remarks

Characters block each other here in the simulation, not in Jolt. Before moving, each character loses the part of its velocity that heads into another character's capsule, and overlapping characters are eased apart. Everyone uses positions from the start of the tick, so the result doesn't depend on update order. Each looks only at the bodies in its own square of a grid and the eight around it (squares as wide as two capsules, so nobody it could touch is further), in the order the bodies were gathered: the same result as looking at every one, for a few each.
