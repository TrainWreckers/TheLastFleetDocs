Remarks

A character's entry holds where it was, how fast it was moving, and when that was recorded. The client extrapolates from there (Predict(ReadOnlySpan<byte>, uint)). So the host leaves the entry unchanged until the client's guess is off by more than matters at that distance (10 cm at the client's feet, more farther away, and three times that for a character the client can't see), or the character has turned more than a few degrees, or changed speed. A soldier walking down a corridor or a Stripper running at you only needs an update when it turns, not twenty times a second. In between, the entry repeats what it said last time, which costs nothing after the delta against the client's last copy. Everything else about a character (health, stance, weapon, death) is always sent fresh.

Remarks

Layout: x, z, yaw, pitch, y (16 bits each, in 3 cm steps and 182nds of a degree), speed (one byte, in tenths of a meter per second), horizontal velocity (two signed bytes, in eighths of a meter per second), and which snapshot it was recorded in (one byte: the tick divided by SnapshotInterval, wrapping every 12 s). Players' entries are always fresh, so they don't include a velocity.
