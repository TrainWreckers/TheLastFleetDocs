Remarks

The station hears the pumping. Once a tank starts, the Director sends a patrol for it out of the nearest closet, plus a horde if the mission's hordes are active. An enemy standing on the hose for CutSeconds cuts it, and pumping stops until a player repairs it. When the hack starts the dropship undocks, and any tank that isn't empty yet stays as it is. Once all the valves on a deep reservoir's pipe are open, the station hears the pipe fill and sends a patrol to the reservoir.

This is one of the command systems and runs after the mission system (it reads and adds to the mission state). Dragging and connecting the hoses is done by the simulation (TankSystem). Doesn't allocate.
