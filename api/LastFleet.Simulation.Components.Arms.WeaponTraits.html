What a weapon does beyond its numbers (see the design doc, "Manufacturers" and "Rarity and affixes"). A trait can be a manufacturer's signature, or the effect of an affix or a relic. The numbers live in WeaponTraitRules.

A suppressor. Soldiers and the horde only hear its shots in the room they're fired in.

A laser sight. Hipfire is tighter, and the beam and dot are drawn.
