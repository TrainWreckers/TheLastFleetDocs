Structs

AcidGlob

A Spitter's glob of acid in flight, with its WorldPosition. It's thrown from Origin at Velocity on LaunchTick, falls under Gravity, and leaves an AcidPool where it lands. Its position can be worked out from those values alone, so clients draw it from them.

AcidPool

A pool of a Spitter's acid on the floor. It burns anyone standing in it who isn't Choir, from StartTick until EndTick. It's there to force players to move.

ChoirUnit

One of the Choir's bigger units (Strippers, Spitters, Weavers, the Herald). It's a character like a soldier (controller, body, health, the Choir's Faction), but driven by the Choir's own simple AI instead of a soldier's. This component holds what kind it is and what it's doing, which the clients can see.

Swarmling

A horde body (the Choir's swarmlings, see the design doc, "Horde simulation"). It isn't a character with a controller and a physics body. It's a small thing that runs over the navigation grid in large numbers. It also has WorldPosition (its feet), ViewAngles (which way it faces), Health and Faction.

SwarmSurface

What a Swarmling is clinging to: the floor, the ceiling (feet on it, hanging down), or a wall it's climbing. Walls are named by the direction they face, since a station's walls line up with the grid. The body sticks out from the surface.
