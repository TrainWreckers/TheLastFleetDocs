A level's mission layout: keycards and locks, the terminal, the extraction point, spawn closets, fallback points where reinforcements can enter when no closet works, hull breaches the horde can come through, and fuel and water tanks to pump into the docked dropship.

Remarks

If the dropship docked, it also has the hold (the spot it's reached from, plus the cargo chute the player looks at to unload) and the airlock's outer hatch (which the player looks at to leave early). A level laid out by a plan can also have escape pods, doors through its bulkheads that seal compartments off (BulkheadPlan), and windows onto space that can be blown out (WindowPlan), sealing their compartment for good with PermanentChance when Walk says the mission can still be finished without it.
