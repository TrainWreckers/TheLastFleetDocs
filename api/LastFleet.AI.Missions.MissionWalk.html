Whether the mission can still be finished with some compartments, or rooms, lost (docs/plans/structure-first.md, decision 8): from the insertion, through doorways whose rooms are both kept, each gate passable once its keycard's room has been reached and the way out once the objective has, can the objective and then an extraction be reached? A breach seals its compartment for good only when this holds without it and every compartment already lost, so two losses can never together lock the squad out, and keycards stay reachable whatever is sealed.
