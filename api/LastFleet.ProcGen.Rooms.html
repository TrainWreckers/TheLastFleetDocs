Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Rooms

Classes

Footprint

The grid cells a room occupies (each CellSize square and one story tall), in the room's own coordinates: X along its width, Z along its depth, and Level for its stories starting at 0. A kit module's footprint is a rectangle. A room built from a shape recipe can be any connected shape (an L, a U, a cross).

RoomArchetype

A kind of room the generator builds from designer data (archetypes.json). It sets the room's shape, the content tags it carries (which placement rules match on), the mission roles it can play, the surface style it's built and dressed in, and how it's furnished. A mission template lists the archetypes its built rooms are picked from, each picked by its weight.

ShapeBuilder

Makes a room's footprint from a ShapeRecipe and a random stream: a rectangle, an L, a T, a U, a cross, a blob, or a designer's stamp as drawn. The result is one story, with every cell connected to the others through shared sides, and its bounds start at cell (0, 0). The same recipe and stream give the same shape on every machine. Shapes are always generated in one orientation; the layout rotates them.

ShapeRecipe

How a room's footprint is made (ShapeBuilder): its kind, how big its bounds can be, and how much of them it leaves out. Everything is in grid cells (4 m), picked from the seed within the given ranges.

StoryFloor

The floor of one story of a tall room, above its ground story (VerticalSpec). Holds what kind of floor it is, how far in from the walls it reaches, and how the stairs climb to it from a floor below.

VerticalSpec

Describes a room more than one story tall (ParametricRoom): a floor, or no floor, for each story above the ground story, from the bottom up. Each floor is reached by stairs from a floor below it (the nearest one where they fit, otherwise one further down, climbing through the open stories in between). Edges over open space get railings, and doorways go where a floor reaches the wall. A story with no floor leaves the room open through it. For example, a three-story room with no middle floor has just a ground floor and a top floor.

Enums

ShapeKind

The kinds of shape a room's footprint can be built from (ShapeBuilder).

StairKind

How the stairs up to a tall room's floor climb (StoryFloor), one story at a time. A climb of more than one story (from a floor two or more stories below, with the stories in between open) stacks them, with a landing at each story.

UpperFloorKind

The kind of floor a story of a tall room has above its ground story (StoryFloor).
