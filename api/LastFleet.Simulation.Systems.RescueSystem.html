Remarks

Hold Interact within ReachMeters of a downed teammate to revive them over ReviveSeconds, with the weapon lowered. Letting go pauses the revive, and its progress slowly decays. Press Drag to grab a downed teammate and pull them along slowly, with only the sidearm available. Press it again to let go. Dragging doesn't stop the bleed-out.

Runs after the life system (which downs players) and before movement, so draggers are slowed on the same tick.
