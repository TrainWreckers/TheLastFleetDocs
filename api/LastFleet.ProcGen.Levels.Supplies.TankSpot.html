A storage tank of fuel or water in the level (its model is baked into the level mesh) that the squad empties into the docked dropship. Its hose runs back through the station to the airlock's port and gets pumped while the ship is docked.

Parameters

Position Vector3

The middle of its base. For a reservoir outlet, where it meets its wall.

Valve Vector3

Where the hose comes off, on the floor in front of it. This is where players pick up the hose.

Supply string

What it holds, by item id ("fuel" or "water").

Amount int

How much it holds right now (zero for a tank found empty).

Deck int

The deck it's on (0 is the first).

Room int

The room it's in.

Capacity int

How much it holds when full (the top of its gauge).

Valves IReadOnlyList<ValveSpot>

For a deep reservoir outlet: the valves along its pipe, the reservoir's own valve first. All of them have to be opened before the hose can be taken (Room is then the outlet's room). Null for a tank.

Reservoir (Vector3 Position, int Room)?

For a reservoir outlet: the middle of the reservoir's base, and its room.
