Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Styles

Classes

SurfaceStyle

How a room's structure is built and how it looks. The generator builds the walls, floors and the rest from the room's structure markers using its style (LastFleet.ProcGen.Styles.StyleBuilder), and dresses it the same way (ceilings, trims, ducts, lights), so designers never have to model a wall. Styles are designer data in content/defs/procgen/styles.json. A style can inherit anything it doesn't set from another style (its base), and ultimately from Station.

Structs

DuctStyle

A duct under the ceiling along one long wall of a room (not a corridor's or a narrow room's).

HallLook

How a hall built in a style looks (ParametricHall): what stands along it, what kind of floor it has, the floor's light, and accent lighting along the walls and on the structure. The walls and floor plate use the style's Wall and Floor, and the ceiling panels use the style's Lights.

LightStyle

A room's ceiling light panels: whether it has any, how many are dead or flicker, and how bright they are.

PipeStyle

Pipes under the ceiling along the long wall opposite the duct, one under and out from the other.

StyleColor

A color in a style: either a fixed color or the room's deck or role color, multiplied by Shade (0.5 is half as bright).

SurfaceLook

How a surface looks: its color (linear RGB) and its material (an id in content/defs/materials.json).

TrimStyle

A band round a room's walls on its ground story, broken at its doorways: a baseboard, a stripe.

Enums

ColorSource

Where a style gets its color from (StyleColor).

HallAccents

Extra lights a hall built in a style can have besides its floor light (Accents). Flags, so any combination works. Each piece is its own light, and it's broken up wherever there's a doorway.

HallFloor

The kind of floor a hall has (ParametricHall), and how it's lit.

HallStructure

What stands along a hall (ParametricHall).
