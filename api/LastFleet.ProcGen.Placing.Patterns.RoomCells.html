A room as furniture patterns see it: its floor in half-meter cells (the cells of its LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Reach.ReachGrid, with a ring of one more around them), each a wall, a doorway, floor or the walkway between its doorways, and what stands on it so far.

Remarks

The walkway is the way from the first doorway to each of the others, turning as little as it can, widened by a cell each side, plus the lanes kept open in from each doorway. Patterns put nothing on it, and its middle is reserved on the reach grid so nothing a rule places later does either.
