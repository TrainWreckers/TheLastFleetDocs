Where a smooth hall (HallBand: a ring's bend, a bridge on the slant) goes through a doorway between two of its stretches, as a bulkhead crosses it: the stretch of the doorway's grid line inside the hall, which is wider than a doorway where the hall runs on a slant, and the hall's middle and direction there. A door there stands square across the hall, from wall to wall (Door(string, DoorKind, Vector3)), not along the grid.
