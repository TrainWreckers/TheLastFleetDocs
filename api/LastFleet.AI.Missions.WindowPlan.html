Room int

The room it's in, by layout index, or -1 if not known.

RoomSeals bool

True if its room can be sealed alone: a room (not a hall) whose every doorway has its own door (RoomDoorPlan). Otherwise a breach seals the whole compartment.

RoomMin Vector3

The low corner of its room's bounds, for whether anyone is in it.

RoomMax Vector3

The high corner of its room's bounds.
