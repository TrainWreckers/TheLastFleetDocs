Class Footprint

The grid cells a room occupies (each CellSize square and one story tall), in the room's own coordinates: X along its width, Z along its depth, and Level for its stories starting at 0. A kit module's footprint is a rectangle. A room built from a shape recipe can be any connected shape (an L, a U, a cross).
