Remarks

When a mission launches (Launch(FleetState, PointOfInterest)), the fleet jumps to the system chosen on the star map if it has the fuel, which widens the gap on the Scourge. If the Scourge has caught it, that's an emergency jump at double cost. Every ship and person also uses up supplies according to the supply rules (FleetSupplies, supplies.json), and shortages cost people, morale and ships.

After the mission (AfterMission(FleetState, Difficulty, bool, in FleetHaul, float)), won or lost, whatever the squad brought back goes into the stores and the Scourge closes in based on the time spent. A mission at the sector's gate takes the fleet through into the next sector. The Scourge's distance paces the missions: the Choir only shows up once it's close, and is aboard from the start once it has caught the fleet. Deterministic from the run's seed and mission count.

Pacing(FleetState, Difficulty)

Returns the pacing for the next mission, based on how close the Scourge is. No Choir while it's far behind. Once it's near, the Choir comes only after the hack starts, then one deck earlier for each step closer, and it's aboard from the start once the Scourge has caught the fleet. The soldiers' Director still gets quicker as the run goes on.
