Enum BarkKind

Namespace
LastFleet.AI.Barks
Assembly
LastFleet.AI.dll

What a soldier calls out. Every bark is triggered by a real decision or event, never by a timer, so listening to the squad tells the player what it's about to do.

public enum BarkKind : byte

Fields

None = 0

No bark (the default).

Contact = 1

Spotted an enemy.

LostContact = 2

The enemy broke line of sight, and the soldier is heading to where he was.

Searching = 3

Reached the last known position and found nothing.

StandDown = 4

Gave up the search.

FlankingLeft = 5

Took the flanker role and is moving around the enemy's left (from the squad's point of view).

FlankingRight = 6

Took the flanker role and is moving around the enemy's right.

CoveringFire = 7

The suppressor opened covering fire.

MovingUp = 8

Decided to advance to closer cover.

Pushing = 9

Decided to rush the enemy.

TargetReloading = 10

Saw the enemy reloading, and is pushing because of it.

FallingBack = 11

Decided to fall back to farther cover, or the squad is retreating.

Reloading = 12

Started reloading during a fight.

FragOut = 13

Threw a grenade.

GrenadeWarning = 14

An enemy grenade landed close, and the soldier is running from it.

ManDown = 15

A squadmate died.

Pinned = 16

Near misses have the soldier pinned down.

Choir = 17

Spotted the Choir (the horde, or its soldiers).
