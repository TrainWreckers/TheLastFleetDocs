Class VerticalSpec

Describes a room more than one story tall (ParametricRoom): a floor, or no floor, for each story above the ground story, from the bottom up. Each floor is reached by stairs from a floor below it (the nearest one where they fit, otherwise one further down, climbing through the open stories in between). Edges over open space get railings, and doorways go where a floor reaches the wall. A story with no floor leaves the room open through it. For example, a three-story room with no middle floor has just a ground floor and a top floor.
