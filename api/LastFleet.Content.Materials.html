Namespace LastFleet.Content.Materials

Classes

FixtureGlow

Works out how bright a light fixture's glass glows. A light and the glass it shines from are treated as one thing, so the glass takes the light's color, and its brightness is the light's strength spread over the glass area (a small lens on the same light glows brighter than a long panel).

MaterialLibrary

The surface materials every model is drawn with (content/defs/materials.json). It's a fixed library, tuned in Studio and picked in Blender, so paint looks like paint on every gun, prop and wall. Each vertex stores its material's index into this library (Material), and the renderer reads the library as a table.

SurfaceMaterial

A material in the library (content/defs/materials.json, see MaterialLibrary). It describes how a surface reacts to light. The color comes from the model; the material says whether it's paint or bare metal, rough or polished.

SightWindow

What a sight's glass shows (Sight): the reticle of a red dot or holographic sight. It's drawn only where the eye looks along the aim (as if projected at infinity). The rest of the glass is clear.
