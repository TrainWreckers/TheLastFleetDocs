Parameters

Panels bool

Whether the ceiling has light panels. A dim service way may only have its floor glow.

DeadChance float

The fraction of panels that are dead. Each room always keeps one lit.

FlickerChance float

The fraction of panels that flicker.

Strength float

How bright each panel's light is, applied to the room's light color.

DeadPanel Vector3

The diffuser color of a dead panel.

Housing Vector3

The color of a panel's housing, which is what's left when the glass is shot out.
