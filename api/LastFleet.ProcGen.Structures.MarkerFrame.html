Where a piece of structure is and which way it faces: a point on its surface, the direction into the room (Inward), and the direction along it (Along). Walls get built and props get placed in this frame, so nothing generated ever has to be rotated by hand.

Remarks

The frame's third axis, Across, is Inward cross Along. A wall's frame comes from OnWall(Vector3, Vector3), which points Along to the right as seen from inside the room. That makes Across point up, so a point given as (along, up, out from the wall) lands where you'd expect.
