The finishing details of a built room (ParametricRoom) beyond its footprint: its surface style, its furnishing, how much its corners are cut, and whether pillars hold up its ceiling.

public RoomFinish(SurfaceStyle? Look = null, Furnishing? Furniture = null, float Chamfer = 0, bool Pillars = false, VerticalSpec? Vertical = null, IReadOnlyList<Furnishing>? Furnishings = null)

Parameters

Look SurfaceStyle

The surface style for its floor and walls. Null uses the station's style.

Furniture Furnishing

The furnishing to use. If null, one is picked from Furnishings using the room's seed.

Chamfer float

How much to cut its corners at 45°, in meters (Sizes). Only applies to corners that have the room on one side only and no doorway on either wall.

Pillars bool

If true, a floor-to-ceiling pillar stands at every interior cell corner (where four of its cells meet), as long as it doesn't block the path between its doorways.

Vertical VerticalSpec

Its upper stories, if it's a tall room (LastFleet.ProcGen.Modules.TallRoom): whether each story has a floor, the stairs up to each floor, railings and doorways. A floor whose stairs don't fit anywhere is dropped. If none of its floors can be reached, the room is built one story tall.

Furnishings IReadOnlyList<Furnishing>

The furnishings to pick from, in order, when no furnishing is given. Null uses the built-in ones (BuiltInPlacements).
