Class RingSplit

Namespace
LastFleet.ProcGen.Plans
Assembly
LastFleet.ProcGen.dll

A ring part cut into sections by angle, when each part is its own section (ByParts): a bulkhead out from its center at each of Quarters, so a ring station's ring is several sections, each its own zone, rather than one. The first piece round from the first quarter keeps the part's own section; the others get sections of their own after every part's, with the part's district.

Remarks

A ring is only cut where it runs square to the grid (east, south, west and north of its center), so its ring hall crosses each bulkhead on a single cell's edge and its pressure door stands straight across it.
