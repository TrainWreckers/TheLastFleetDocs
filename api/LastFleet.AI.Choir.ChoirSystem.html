The Choir's bigger bodies (ChoirUnit): characters with a simple mind of their own, not a soldier's. Each goes for the nearest of anyone not of the Choir (players and soldiers alike), by the navigation grid or straight at them once in the open, and attacks after a wind-up that warns: Strippers close in and strike everyone and everything in front of them (cover and breakable walls included, going through what stands between them and their target); Spitters keep their distance and lob acid that leaves a burning pool (AcidGlob, AcidPool).

Remarks

A late system, after the horde: it writes each body's command for the next tick (as the soldiers' AI acts on the last tick's world), and its blows and acid are logged as hits after this tick's shots cleared the log. Deterministic; allocation-free after the first ticks.
