Remarks

A lane costs the network's price for its kind, either per centimeter (rounded) or once for a wall or lintel crossing, plus Bend wherever the route turns. An open-ceiling span longer than OpenSpan is never used, and neither is a lane already used by a different kind of network (LaneUsage). A lane already used by the same kind costs a quarter less, so same-kind networks bundle together. If the route goes through more service cells than ServiceCells, it's searched again with service cells disabled.

The heuristic is the axis-aligned (Manhattan) distance to the goal, since every lane runs along an axis, times the cheapest per-centimeter cost of any lane. That never overestimates the real cost, so the route found is the cheapest.
