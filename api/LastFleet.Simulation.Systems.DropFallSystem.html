Namespace

Makes loot fall when whatever it was resting on breaks (docs/plans/furniture.md). Every weapon, pile, case and cargo crate resting on a prop that broke this tick (inside its bounds, from its base to a little above its top) starts falling, and keeps falling under gravity until a ray finds something to land on. Positions go out in snapshots as normal, so clients see it fall.

Remarks

Runs after every system that breaks props (weapons, grenades, the horde's blows) and before the tick ends, while the tick's DestructionLog still holds the breaks. It's deterministic: it uses rays against the level's collision, which the broken prop no longer blocks.
