What a network's route costs for each kind of lane (LaneKind). Costs are integers so every machine finds the same route. They're charged per meter along a lane, once for each wall or lintel passed through, and once for each bend. A higher cost keeps routes off that kind of lane unless nothing cheaper gets there.
