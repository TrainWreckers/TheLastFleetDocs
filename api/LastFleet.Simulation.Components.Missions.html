Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Components.Missions

Structs

Breach

A hull breach: a patched plate in the station's outer hull. Like a ClosetDoor, it sits on the same entity as an unbreakable Destructible (the plate). The horde blows it open (marked broken, collision off) and pours in through the hole behind it, and it stays open. It's replicated through the broken-surface list like any prop.

ClosetDoor

A spawn closet's door. Like a MissionLock, it's a level part on the same entity as an unbreakable Destructible. It's open (marked broken, collision off) while reinforcements leave the closet, and shuts again once the closet is empty. It's replicated through the broken-surface list like any prop.

EscapePod

An escape pod's hatch. Like a ClosetDoor, it's a level part on the same entity as an unbreakable Destructible, but the other way round: open (marked broken, collision off) until the pod launches, then shut for good with whoever was inside.

HullWindow

A window onto space (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10), on the same entity as its pane's Destructible, which holds (takes damage, never breaks: Holds). Its damage shows as cracks, then blows it out: a breach, which the mission's breach system runs.

MissionKey

A keycard lying in the level (with a WorldPosition). The squad picks it up by walking over it.

MissionLock

A locked door: a level part on the same entity as a Destructible that shots can't break. Its keycard opens it by marking the part broken and turning its collision off, so clients see it and walk through it the same way as a broken prop. Restarting the fight closes it again.

MissionState

The mission's state, kept on one entity. It's small, so it's replicated whole to co-op clients in every snapshot, and it's hashed in the simulation checksum. Keycard and lock ids start at 1, with one bit each in KeysHeld and LocksOpen.

RoomDoorState

A room's own door in a plan's level (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10), on the same entity as its door's Destructible: open is broken, as for any door, so clients see it the same way. It slides open for whoever comes near, or (ByHand) is opened and shut by hand; the swarm batters one opened by hand until it's Wrecked, open for good. A breach in either of its rooms seals it shut.

SealableDoor

A door through a bulkhead, between compartments A and B, that shuts while either is sealed: on the same entity as its door's Destructible (a pressure door's, or a gate's beside its MissionLock). Open is broken, as for any door, so clients see it the same way.

WindowPart

One of a window's parts that the mission shows as its breach goes (HullWindow): on the same entity as the part's Destructible, which never breaks but is marked broken while the part is hidden, so clients see it come and go the same way as anything broken.
