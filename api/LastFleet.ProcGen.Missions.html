Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Missions

Classes

AirlockModels

The models of the airlock's fittings in the hull (a mission template's "airlock"): the dropship's intake, where a tank's hose couples, and its cargo chute, each a station prop ("file/Object").

AlertSettings

How a mission's station alert level builds up (its template's "alert"): how it rises over time and with noise, how little it drops, and what it does to patrols and the horde. The fight's Director reads these (see its AlertTuning).

BoardingTargets

Where a boarding action's targets are in a ship (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"): its gun batteries, its reactor and its fire suppression controls. Read the same from the level on every machine, so the host's mission and everyone's markers agree.

BreachRule

How a plan mission's breaches go (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10; the breaches block of a template's plan): the chance a breach seals its compartment for good when the mission can still be finished without it, and the districts never lost, whose compartments only ever seal for a while. How tough windows are, the pull and the timers are every mission's (breaches.json).

EarlyExtractionSettings

Settings for leaving early on the docked dropship (a mission template's "earlyExtraction"): whether the squad can, how long they hold at the airlock to leave, and what the fleet needs to get for it to count as a success. That requisition is the supplies running short, topped up to ReserveJumps jumps of reserve, with some minimum amount either way.

ExitRule

How a plan mission's squad gets on or off: the kinds of exit it would rather use, in order. The first kind the level has wins.

FleetRule

The fleet seen from a plan's windows (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 10): how many ships hang in space round the level, and how far off, set in the mission template's plan (fleet).

GenerationSettings

The settings a lobby can change on a mission template before a level is generated: how many decks, how many soldiers, how much loot, how many stories, and how many rooms are built from the seed. They're encoded in the level's name (Encode()), so every co-op peer generates the same level from the name alone.

KeycardRule

Where a plan mission's keycards go: one for each gate the hack doesn't open, in a room of the zone before its gate, off the way through.

KeycardStep

The keycard for each deck's gate into the next (but the gate the hack opens), on a branch off the deck's way through, sometimes behind a room to explore: before its lock, so the level can always be finished. On every deck.

LevelRecipe

How a level's mission graph is grown, deck by deck (MissionGraph). It's a list of steps in order: first the main path (MainPathStep), then the keycard (KeycardStep), then any side and loot rooms. Every step is applied to each deck in turn, so the level is always finishable by construction: each keycard sits on its deck before the gate it opens. This is a mission template's recipe. A template without one grows from its room counts (Of(MissionTemplate)).

LoopRules

How a level's loops get made, beyond how many each zone gets (Loops). Sets how far apart in the room tree a loop's two ends have to be, how long a corridor routed around corners for a loop can be, what fraction of rooms should end up on a loop, the most that should be dead ends, and how many one-way shortcuts cross each gate between zones. Loops keep getting added until both the loop and dead-end targets are met, or no more fit.

LootRoomsStep

Loot rooms, each off any room of the deck but its ends (and other loot rooms).

LootSettings

Weapon loot settings for a mission's level (design doc, "Procedural weapons and loot"). Loot tables are only named here, not loaded; the game rolls them. Item level goes up by one per zone, so deeper decks have better weapons. Loot rooms hold caches. On top of that, the Director hides a few stashes in each zone, placed to reward going out of the way or taking on a guarded room.

MainPathStep

Builds the main path through each deck: the first room (the insertion, or a transit set piece behind the gate), some rooms along the way, then the objective and arena on the second-to-last deck and extraction at the end of the last. It's always the recipe's first step and runs on every deck.

MissionGraph

Pass 1 (design doc): the mission as a tree of rooms starting from the insertion, with key and lock pairs. It's grown by the template's recipe (LevelRecipe), applying each step in turn on each deck, so it's always completable by construction: each keycard sits off the path before its lock.

MissionTemplate

A mission template (design doc, "Mission types" and "Pass 1: mission graph"), read from content/defs/missions/*.json. It sets how many rooms of each kind, how many key and lock pairs, loops and soldiers, and how many stories the level can climb through (1 keeps it flat).

ObjectiveRule

What a plan mission's squad comes for and where it is: a room of the zone before the last (docs/plans/structure- first.md, "Objective kinds"). Whatever its kind, finishing it opens the gate into the last zone, where extraction is.

PlanRecipe

How a mission reads itself into a plan's level (docs/plans/structure-first.md, "The mission recipe"). The level's structure is built first, so there's nothing to grow: these are rules for choosing among the rooms it has.

PodRule

A plan mission's escape pods: bays on the hull, each with a pod or two, that take one or two players off at any time (never a crate). Whoever gets off by whatever way, the squad ends up together.

RecipeStep

One step of a level's recipe (LevelRecipe): what it adds to each deck's rooms, in the recipe's order.

ReservoirSettings

Settings for deep reservoirs of fuel or water (a mission template's "reservoir"). A reservoir is a big tank deeper in the station than a hose can reach. It's piped back to an outlet near the airlock through valves the squad has to open (one at the reservoir and some along the way) before the hose can be run from the outlet to the dropship.

RoomRule

Which of a plan's rooms a mission rule is after: those with any of Any's tags, from their archetype (archetypes.json), a tag also matching every tag under it (room.storage takes room.storage.armory). With no tags, every room.

SideRoomsStep

Adds side rooms to explore. Each is a dead end off the deck's main path, sometimes two rooms deep. A one-deck level gets none.

StationSupplies

The supplies a station's cargo can hold, as defined in the game's data (the loot data's fleet supplies, put into use with Use(StationSupplies?); with no data, Shipped). Lists every supply a station can be chosen for (a CargoFocus), each with its color, used for a tank's band and glow when it holds that supply. Which ones are kept in tanks is up to the mission template (TankSupplies). Every co-op peer generates its levels from the same set.

TankKind

A kind of supply tank a station's rooms can have (a mission template's "tankKinds"). It sets the model (a station prop in content/props, LastFleet.ProcGen.Levels.Dressing.StationProps), how much room it takes, which supplies it can hold, and how often it's picked over other kinds that can hold the same supply.

ThreatRule

How hard a plan mission's places are defended, and what that sets (docs/plans/structure-first.md, "Threat drives the fight"; the threat block of a template's plan). Each zone's threat rises from the first to the last, scaled by its district and by what a room is (a security office more, quarters less). While the squad is somewhere, its threat sets how often the Director sends a patrol and how big; the station's alert speeds both on top. Patrols come from the spawn closets beside Sources rooms or on a service way first.
