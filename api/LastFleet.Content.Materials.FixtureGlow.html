Works out how bright a light fixture's glass glows. A light and the glass it shines from are treated as one thing, so the glass takes the light's color, and its brightness is the light's strength spread over the glass area (a small lens on the same light glows brighter than a long panel).

Remarks

Panels, alarm beacons, flashlight lenses and muzzle flashes are all drawn this way, so what blooms on screen and what lights the room always match.

Fields

Intensity

The scale from a light's strength per square meter of glass to how bright the glass glows. A station panel at its usual strength (0.5) glows about 3.2, the same as its glass did before lights and glass were merged.

public const float Intensity = 3.584

Remarks

The glass and its light always share color, dimming and failure, but glass as bright as the room lighting needs would bloom out the whole room. This value keeps station rooms about as bright as when panels were point lights with a gentle falloff. Strips and channels shine exactly as bright as their glass glows (LightFor(Vector3, float)).

MaxGlow

The maximum glow for any glass (on its brightest channel), no matter how small it is. Past this the bloom would swallow the view. The light's hue is preserved.

public const float MaxGlow = 6
