Remarks

The squad loads supplies into it, then calls a drone and holds the pod until the drone lands and takes it to the fleet. The fleet keeps what was in it even if the mission fails afterward. If it's left to the enemy, it gets overrun and its contents are lost. The mission's AI runs the defense.
