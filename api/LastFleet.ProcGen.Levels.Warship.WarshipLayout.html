What a boarding action's warship has aboard (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"): its gun batteries, its reactor and its fire suppression controls. The level's rules stand them in the rooms the mission gave them (mission.battery, mission.reactor, mission.suppression: a gun deck each, an engine or reactor room, damage control).
