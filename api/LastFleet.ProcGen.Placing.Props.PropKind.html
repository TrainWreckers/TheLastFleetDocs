Enum PropKind

Namespace
LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Props
Assembly
LastFleet.ProcGen.dll

What a prop is in gameplay terms, which decides what placing it creates. Furniture is built into the room. A footlocker, case, cargo crate, supply pile, keycard, ammo crate or tank is a mission object.

public enum PropKind : byte

Fields

Furniture = 0

A piece of furniture built into the room: a solid box, possibly breakable. Items can sit on top of a low one.

Footlocker = 1

A footlocker to search. Its body is built into the level against a wall, and is solid.

Case = 2

A supply case to search, sitting on top of something.

Cargo = 3

A big crate of fleet supplies, carried out with both hands.

Supplies = 4

A pile of supplies, rolled from the mission's pile table.

Keycard = 5

A keycard for a locked gate. One per gate, placed in the rooms on the near side of the lock.

Ammo = 6

An ammo crate.

Tank = 7

A supply tank of the kinds the mission template uses. The template sets how many, and each must be within reach of its hose.

Window = 8

A window onto space, on a wall on the hull: a frame in its color round a pane that shows the sector's sky, its glass from its sill up to its top. Built into the wall's face, it stands in nobody's way.
