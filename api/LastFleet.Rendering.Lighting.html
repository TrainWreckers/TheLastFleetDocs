Namespace LastFleet.Rendering.Lighting

Classes

RectLights

Computes the light a rectangle casts on a surface, the same way the world shader does (rectIrradiance in content/shaders/scene_lights.slang). It uses Lambert's formula for the rectangle's polygon as seen from the surface, after clipping it at the surface's horizon. This is for tests and tools; the shader is what actually lights the frame.

RoomLighting

A level's rooms, as the world shaders use them for lighting. Holds a grid saying which room each cell belongs to (the station's own layout grid, where rooms are made of whole cells), and each room's bounce light for this frame: the light from its active lights, bounced once off its walls.

Structs

FogVolume

A cloud of smoke for one frame, for example from a blast or a breach. It's densest at the center and thins out to nothing at its radius. The froxel grid adds it to the air (FogVolumes).

PointLight

A light for the world shader (112 bytes, matches PointLight in content/shaders/scene_lights.slang). A point light fades to nothing at its radius and only lights what's inside its box (a room), so it doesn't need shadows to keep from leaking into the next room. A spotlight (a flashlight) also only lights inside its cone. A rect light (Rect(Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, Vector3, float, Vector3, Vector3): a panel's glass, a floor channel in a hall, a light strip) shines from one side of a rectangle, as bright as its glass glows, and fades with distance from the nearest point on it.

ReflectionCapture

A request to recapture a room's reflection cube map. It names the room (an index into the frame's Rooms), the lights to capture it with (a range of CaptureLights, which don't cast shadows in the capture), and which level geometry to draw (a range of CaptureRanges: the room's own parts plus what's visible through its doorways, nothing broken). Characters and effects are never captured.

RoomDoorway

A doorway between two rooms (Doorways): which rooms it connects and the box of its opening.

ShadowView

A shadow map for this frame. Its tile in the frame's atlas matches its index in the frame's list. The level part of the map is cached at CacheSlot and only redrawn when Refresh is set (first use, or something in its room changed, like a prop broke or a door opened). It's drawn from the level index ranges ShadowRanges[First .. First + Count]. Characters move, so they're drawn on top every frame when Characters is set.

Enums

Atmosphere

How the air between the camera and the scene is rendered. Either as even haze (the level's haze only, the old distance fog), or through a froxel grid over the view, coarse or fine, where the frame's lights show up as light beams and glows.
