Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Systems

Classes

CargoSystem

Handles cargo crates carried in both hands (Carried, picked up by the weapon pickup system). While carrying, the player is slower, can't sprint or dash, has the weapon lowered, and can't use anything else.

CharacterBodySystem

Moves characters through the level: velocity in, position, collision-adjusted velocity and ground contact out. Also swaps the standing and crouching capsules, keeps a character crouched where it has no room to stand, and keeps characters from walking through each other.

CharacterControllerSystem

Applies each character's PlayerCommand: look first, then the movement model.

CoverSystem

Red Orchestra style cover for soldiers, and low cover and leaning for players. A character takes low cover automatically when it faces something close by.

DestructionSystem

Handles breakable props. Shots (ShotResolutionSystem) and grenades (GrenadeSystem) damage them through this system. When a prop breaks, its collision is switched off so it stops blocking movement, sight and shots, and the break is recorded in the DestructionLog.

DropFallSystem

Makes loot fall when whatever it was resting on breaks (docs/plans/furniture.md). Every weapon, pile, case and cargo crate resting on a prop that broke this tick (inside its bounds, from its base to a little above its top) starts falling, and keeps falling under gravity until a ray finds something to land on. Positions go out in snapshots as normal, so clients see it fall.

EffectSystem

Applies effects to players over time (PlayerEffects).

GrenadeSystem

Frag grenades. Handles throwing them (a fresh press of Grenade throws along the view), flying and bouncing off the level, and exploding when the fuse runs out. Explosions damage the thrower's enemies if there's a clear line from the blast, with damage falling off with distance, and are recorded in the ExplosionLog.

HullPulls

How an open breach (HullPull) drags whoever can see it: like a wind, a drift added to where they move each tick (CharacterBodySystem), so ground friction doesn't eat it and running away still counts. It's strongest at the breach, falling off evenly to nothing at its reach, only in front of the wall it's in and only in sight of it. The same on the host and in a client's prediction, which runs the same body step.

Interaction

Works out what a player is looking at, for interacting with it. A thing counts as looked at when the line of sight from the eye passes within its radius (treating it as an upright column from its base up to its height) and it's close enough to reach.

InventorySystem

Applies the inventory actions (InventoryOp) players send in their commands to their Inventory.

LagCompensation

Lag compensation for co-op: remembers where every character was at the end of each recent tick, so a client's shot is tested against the characters where that client saw them, not where they have moved since.

LifeSystem

Handles health over time: regeneration, being downed, death and respawn. A dead character's input is ignored and its collision body is removed, so others can walk through the corpse. Players with a SpawnPoint respawn after RespawnSeconds with the stock kit (DefaultLoadout(ulong)). The weapons they found and everything in their inventory are left where they died, for them or a teammate to pick up. Soldiers stay dead.

LoadoutSystem

Switches weapons. When a command asks for another slot, the weapon in hand is stowed in its slot and the new one is brought up, and it can't fire until its swap time has passed. A reload in progress is lost, like in Destiny.

MantleSystem

Starts and drives mantles. Runs after the controller, so it can take over this tick's velocity.

PickupSystem

Ammo pickups in the world. Characters with an AmmoDrop leave a pickup where they die, and players who walk over a pickup take it if any of their weapons has room. Dropped pickups disappear after a while; crates respawn.

PingSystem

Pings. Pressing Ping marks whatever is in the middle of the player's view: an enemy there (with a little leeway, so it doesn't need perfect aim), or otherwise the spot where the view hits the level.

RescueSystem

Reviving and dragging downed teammates (design doc, "Downed, revive and drag").

ShotResolutionSystem

Resolves hitscan shots. Casts each of this tick's shots against the level and every living character, applies damage to whatever target or character it hits (including falloff and precision), and records the result in the HitLog.

TankSystem

Handles dragging supply tank hoses (SupplyTank).

TargetSystem

Stands destroyed targets back up, at full health, once their respawn time has passed.

WeaponPickupSystem

Handles everything a player uses with Interact: weapons and items in the world, containers, cargo, pods, supply tanks and the dropship's hold. Most take a press; searching a container and turning a valve take a hold.

WeaponSystem

Runs the weapon model for every armed character and records shots in the ShotLog.

WeaponTraitSystem

Applies weapon trait effects (WeaponTraits) after this tick's shots are resolved.

Structs

HeldEffects

The combined passive effects of everything a player is holding (see Held(in Inventory)).

Interfaces

IShownCharacters

Where each client was shown the other characters. In co-op the host knows this from what it sent each client.

IShownHorde

Where each client was shown the horde. In co-op the host knows this from what it sent each client. A client's shots at swarmlings are resolved against these positions, so it hits what it saw (see LagCompensation).

Enums

PodUse

The result of holding Interact at a cargo pod (see LoadOrCall(ref CargoPod, ref Inventory)).
