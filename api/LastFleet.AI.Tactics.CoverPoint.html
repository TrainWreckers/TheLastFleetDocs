Parameters

Position Vector3

Where to stand (feet position), on the navigation grid.

Facing Vector3

Unit horizontal direction from the spot toward the cover.

Low bool

True for waist-high cover: crouch to hide, stand up to shoot over it. False for full-height cover, where the soldier has to step out to shoot.

Peek Vector3

For full-height cover, the offset to step to for a clear shot past its edge. Zero for low cover, or if there's no such spot.
