Builds a stretch of ring hall smooth (docs/plans/structure-first.md, Phase 7): its cells are still the grid's, but its walls follow its band (HallBand: round a ring's bend, or straight on a slant) as straight sides, each a box turned to its side, so the hall reads as a curve instead of steps. The rooms either side keep their walls on the grid.

Remarks

The floor covers every cell. The walls stand on the band's two edges, clipped to the stretch's own cells. Where a doorway's edge crosses the band (the next stretch, or a door through a bulkhead), the hall runs on through it, and a doorway narrower than the edge gets jambs along the edge either side of it so a shut door seals. Where a doorway is off the band (a room's door), a throat runs from it to the wall: two jambs along the way in, the wall cut between them.

The space between the band and the cells' edges is shut in by the walls either side, so nobody sees or reaches it. Every wall is a box (BoxFit), so it collides as one; nothing is left to the dressing but its ceiling and lights (Band).
