Remarks

Each method works in model space and writes the joints' local transforms back. joints is the pose's model-space transforms (ModelSpace(Span<Matrix4x4>)), and every call keeps it up to date. Nothing here allocates.

Remarks

The middle joint bends the same way the pose already bends it, as long as it's clearly bent and not against pole (in model space). Otherwise it bends toward the pole. The tip keeps its model-space rotation (a hand stays the way it was holding, a foot stays flat).
