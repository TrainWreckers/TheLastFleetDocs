Remarks

A player takes the hose at the valve and drags it back through the station to the docked dropship's port. It's slow, with the weapon lowered, like carrying a crate. The hose is laid along the path they walk (Hose). Once it's coupled, it pumps into the dropship, and whatever is pumped belongs to the fleet even if the mission fails afterward.

Enemies standing on the hose cut it, and a player has to mend it. When the hack begins, the dropship casts off and whatever is left in the tanks stays behind. A deep reservoir is a tank at its outlet by the airlock. It's Sealed until the valves on its pipe are opened.

TankSystem handles the hose. The mission's AI handles the pumping and the station's response.
