Class Skeleton

Namespace

The joints that drive a skinned model (a glTF skin: the armature made in Blender on a rig), ordered so each comes after its parent. It holds their names, their rest pose relative to their parents, and the inverse bind matrices that take a vertex from model space to each joint.
