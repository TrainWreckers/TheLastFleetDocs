Settings for deep reservoirs of fuel or water (a mission template's "reservoir"). A reservoir is a big tank deeper in the station than a hose can reach. It's piped back to an outlet near the airlock through valves the squad has to open (one at the reservoir and some along the way) before the hose can be run from the outlet to the dropship.

It's piped back to an outlet near the airlock through valves the squad has to open (one at the reservoir and some along the way) before the hose can be run from the outlet to the dropship.

Parameters

Chance float

The chance a station has reservoirs (a station too small for one never has any). Not rolled when they're Required.

Amount (int Min, int Max)

The min and max amount a reservoir holds.

Junctions (int Min, int Max)

The min and max number of valves on the pipe between it and the outlet (at most 3).

ValveSeconds float

How long Interact has to be held to open a valve, in seconds. The station hears it turning.

PumpRate float

How fast it pumps into the dropship once open, in units per second.
