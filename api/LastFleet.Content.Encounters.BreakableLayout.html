Remarks

A part named after a breakable plus Separator (Break_Chair12/Shape, a model's drawn shape) that comes after it in the mesh belongs to it (to the last breakable with that name before it). It breaks, hides and gets repaired along with it (Pieces), and isn't a breakable of its own.
