Namespace LastFleet.Client.Fleet

Classes

Armory

The armory's workbench and locker (design doc, "The armoury"): the rules for what the squad does with the fleet's weapons and parts between missions. Store and take, fit and remove parts at once, dismantle into raw material for the refinery, rework with refined material, and fabricate into the locker.

LockerHands

Between the armory's locker and what a player carries (design doc, "The armoury"): the weapon in their hands or a part from their pack into the locker, or something from the locker into their hands or pack. The host does it on its world, so everyone sees it.

Refinery

The fleet's refinery and the armory's fabricator, driven by the loot data (grades, refinery tiers, recipes, fabrication). Raw materials brought back go into the fleet's stores (Deliver(FleetState, in FleetHaul)). Each jump the refinery turns them into graded material, as many batches as its tier allows, in the grades it can make. Upgrading the refinery costs fleet supplies. The fabricator spends graded material on a weapon or a part at the grade's item level.
