Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Looting

Classes

CacheLoot

A cache of weapons in each room.

ItemTables

The item tables a room's piles, footlockers, cases and cargo are rolled from.

LootAssignment

The loot that rooms matching a tag query get (loot.json): a cache of weapons, a chance at their deck's stashes, the tables their piles, containers and cargo are rolled from, and item levels added to what's rolled there.

LootData

Where a level's loot goes (loot.json): the tag-based assignments (LootAssignment) in order, plus how many stashes each deck gets when the template's even split won't do. Without a loot.json, the built-in assignments (BuiltIn(TagRegistry)) put loot where the mission template always has.

LootTables

Item tables, by what's rolled from them. A null table means the mission template's table is used.

RoomLoot

The loot a room gets, with its assignments resolved against the mission template (Of(LootData, TagSet, LootSettings)): its cache, its stash weight and table, the tables its piles, containers and cargo are rolled from, and the item levels added to each.

StashLoot

How likely a room is to hold one of its deck's stashes. The Director hides stashes where they reward going out of the way, so the weight goes up for each room deeper off the main path than the first, and for each soldier guarding the room.
