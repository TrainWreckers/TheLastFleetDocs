A mission template (design doc, "Mission types" and "Pass 1: mission graph"), read from content/defs/missions/*.json. It sets how many rooms of each kind, how many key and lock pairs, loops and soldiers, and how many stories the level can climb through (1 keeps it flat).

Remarks

With more than one zone, a level is a chain of zones (station decks), each its own small mission: main path rooms, side rooms to explore, loot, and the keycard for the gate into the next zone, whose first room is a transit set piece. The objective and the arena are in the second-to-last zone. The gate into the last zone opens when the hack is done, and extraction is at its far end. Room counts, side rooms, loot, soldiers and loops are then per zone, and Locks isn't used, since every gate is a lock.
