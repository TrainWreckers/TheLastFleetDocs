Enum FleetRequestKind

Namespace
LastFleet.Networking.Lobby
Assembly
LastFleet.Networking.dll

What a joined player asks the host to do with the fleet. Everything at home is shared, so anyone in the lobby can ask; the host checks the request against its fleet and carries it out.

public enum FleetRequestKind : byte

Fields

SetCourse = 1

Vote for a course: a system one jump away, or through the gate. Value is the system's id, or StarMap.GateExit. The course set is the one most of the squad picked.

UpgradeRefinery = 2

Upgrade the refinery to its next tier, paid from the fleet's supplies.

Fabricate = 3

Fabricate something into the armory's locker. Text is the fabrication's id.

VoteJump = 4

Vote to jump on the course set (Value 1), or take the vote back (0).

PickPoint = 5

Pick the point of interest in the fleet's system the squad drops to next. Value is its id.

StoreWeapon = 6

Put the weapon in this player's hands in the locker (never their last).

StorePart = 7

Put the part in this player's inventory slot Value in the locker.

TakeItem = 8

Take locker item Value: a weapon into this player's hands (the one held goes in the locker if their hands are full), a part into their pack.

FitPart = 9

Fit locker part Extra to locker weapon Value.

RemovePart = 10

Take the part fitted in slot Extra off locker weapon Value.

Dismantle = 11

Dismantle locker item Value into raw material for the refinery.

Rework = 12

Rework locker item Value: a weapon up a few levels, a part tuned.
