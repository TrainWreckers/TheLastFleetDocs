The scope view for this frame (the Scope parameter of FrameParameters; docs/plans/weapons.md phase 2, docs/plans/combat.md). The tonemap pass draws its lens over the zoomed scene, with its reticle at the aim point. Looking through a scope's eyepiece (Window), the lens is that window, and the rest of the frame (the scope's body round it, the world past it) is left as drawn, as Modern Warfare's scopes are. Without one, the lens is a circle of its own and the rest of the screen is blurred and darkened.

The scope view for this frame (the Scope parameter of FrameParameters; docs/plans/weapons.md phase 2, docs/plans/combat.md). The tonemap pass draws its lens over the zoomed scene, with its reticle at the aim point. Looking through a scope's eyepiece (Window), the lens is that window, and the rest of the frame (the scope's body round it, the world past it) is left as drawn, as Modern Warfare's scopes are. Without one, the lens is a circle of its own and the rest of the screen is blurred and darkened.

public ScopeLens(float Amount, Vector2 Offset, ScopeReticle Reticle, float Window = 0)

Parameters

Amount float

0 to 1, from no scope to fully up to the eye. The lens opens as the scope comes up.

Offset Vector2

How far the lens is off the aim point as the gun sways and kicks, in screen heights (+X right, +Y up). The reticle stays on the aim point, where the shots go. The lens moves around it, and its edge shadows the side it's moving away from.

Reticle ScopeReticle

The reticle drawn on the aim point.

Window float

The radius of the eyepiece's window on the screen, in screen heights, when looking through one; 0 for a lens drawn over the screen (Radius).

Fields
