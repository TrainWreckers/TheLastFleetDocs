A creature made and animated in Blender (content/models, docs/blender/creatures.md): its meshes skinned to an armature and its actions, baked when loaded into where every vertex is on every frame, so the GPU can draw hundreds of them at once, each on its own frame, with no skinning of its own.

Remarks

The file's meshes are the body near (Lods[0]) and, those named *_LOD1, far away (Lods[1]; the near one again when there is none). Each animation is sampled at FramesPerSecond, from its start to its end inclusive. Translation, rotation (slerped) and scale of any node may be animated, with linear or step interpolation (a cubic spline is followed through its keys, linearly). Skinned meshes follow their joints (up to four a vertex); other meshes follow their own node.

Faces are flat: each triangle has its own three vertices, and its normal is worked out again on every frame.
