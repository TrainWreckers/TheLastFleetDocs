ScopeLens

The scope view for this frame (the Scope parameter of FrameParameters; docs/plans/weapons.md phase 2, docs/plans/combat.md). The tonemap pass draws its lens over the zoomed scene, with its reticle at the aim point. Looking through a scope's eyepiece (Window), the lens is that window, and the rest of the frame (the scope's body round it, the world past it) is left as drawn, as Modern Warfare's scopes are. Without one, the lens is a circle of its own and the rest of the screen is blurred and darkened.
