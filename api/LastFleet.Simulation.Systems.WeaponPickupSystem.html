Remarks

A player standing by a WeaponDrop or an ItemDrop, looking at it (Interaction), and pressing Interact picks it up. A weapon goes into a free slot, or replaces the weapon in hand, which is left where the new one was. Items go into the inventory as far as they fit, and the rest stays on the ground.

A closed Container is opened by holding Interact for HoldSeconds (it's searched), and its contents are laid out on top of it. A CargoCrate is picked up in both hands (see CargoSystem). At a loading CargoPod, holding loads the fleet supplies from the pack into it, or if the pack has none and the pod has some, calls the drone for it. At a SupplyTank's valve, or the end of its hose where it's lying, holding picks up the hose to drag (TankSystem). At a cut in the hose, it repairs it. At a shut valve on a deep reservoir's pipe, holding for its ValveSeconds opens it. At the docked dropship's hold (the cargo chute by the hatch, Chute), it unloads the fleet supplies from the pack into it.

When several things are in reach and in view, the one looked at most directly wins. While carrying a crate, nothing else can be used.

Runs after the loadout system and before the weapon system, so a picked-up weapon starts raising on the same tick. A player who is reviving a teammate (weapon holstered), downed or dead can't pick anything up. Doesn't allocate, apart from the items an opened container lays out.
