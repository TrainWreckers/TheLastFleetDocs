Namespace LastFleet.ProcGen.Layouts

Classes

Grid

The module grid: 4 m cells, sides and quarter turns.

LayoutSolver

Places a mission graph's rooms on the grid, then adds loops and one-way shortcuts.

LevelLayout

The result of pass 2 (design doc): the mission's rooms placed on the grid, joined by doorways, with loops.

LinearityReport

How linear a level is, so the designer can compare it with its recipe (LoopRules).

RoomTags

Works out the tags the generator gives each placed room (BuiltInTags): what the room is for (role.) and where it is in the layout. That covers its deck (deck.), whether it's one of the kit's own mission rooms (type.kit), and whether it's on the mission's main path, a dead end, or on a loop. A built room also gets the content tags its archetype gave it, and a boarding action's rooms what the warship has there (mission.battery, mission.reactor, mission.suppression).

Structs

Doorway

An open (or locked) passage between two placed rooms, through their matching connectors.

GridDoor

A doorway in level grid terms: its center in half cells, the side it faces out of, and its story.

HullEdge

A side of a layout cell that faces out of the hull (a plan's level, Hull): past it is space, which a window there looks out on.

PlacedRoom

A module placed in a level.

Placement

A module placed in a level. It's rotated by quarter turns, the rotated footprint's corner is at cell (X, Z), and its ground floor is on story Level.

Plug

An unused connector that gets walled up.

Side

A side of a room module's footprint. North is -Z (the direction yaw 0 faces).
