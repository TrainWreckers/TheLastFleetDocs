A horde body (the Choir's swarmlings, see the design doc, "Horde simulation"). It isn't a character with a controller and a physics body. It's a small thing that runs over the navigation grid in large numbers. It also has WorldPosition (its feet), ViewAngles (which way it faces), Health and Faction.
