Namespace LastFleet.Simulation.Effects

Classes

CallPodAction

Drops a cargo pod (CargoPod) in front of the player, but only while a mission is still going ("do": "callPod").

ChangeStatAction

Changes Stat by By, as a fraction of the stat (0.25 is a quarter more). When used, it lasts Seconds. As a passive, it lasts while its item is held and Seconds is 0.

EffectAction

One thing an effect does (one entry in its "actions" list in effects.json, picked by its "do"). Each kind is its own record holding the values it needs, and EffectRunner carries them out.

EffectCueLog

The effect cues raised this tick (EffectCueRecord). It's cleared every tick, like the explosions, and the client plays them.

EffectDefinition

What an item does (content/defs/loot/effects.json): when it happens (Trigger) and what it does (Actions, in order). A consumable item names one of these.

EffectRegistry

The effects currently in play (EffectDefinition). Once the loot data's effects.json is put in play with Use(IReadOnlyList<EffectDefinition>?), those are used. Otherwise it's the shipped ones (Shipped), which match the repository's data. Items refer to effects by id.

EffectRunner

Runs an effect (EffectDefinition) on a player. It can check whether using the effect now would do anything, and it applies the effect's actions in order. Anything that needs the world (like dropping a pod) is handed back for the caller to do.

FlashAction

A burst of light at the player's position. Its color is Color (each channel 0 to 1) times Brightness, it reaches Radius meters, and it fades over Seconds. It's purely visual.

GiveArmorAction

Gives Amount armor, up to Max. If the player already has more than Max, nothing is taken away. Armor absorbs damage after the shield and before health, and it doesn't regenerate.

HealAction

Heals Amount health, up to max health ("do": "heal").

PlaceDeviceAction

Places a device in front of the player, on the wall ahead or on the floor. This is the breaching charge.

RefillAmmoAction

Adds Magazines magazines' worth of ammo to every weapon's reserve ("do": "refillAmmo").

ShieldAction

A shield of Amount that absorbs damage first. It regenerates at PerSecond after DelaySeconds without taking damage.

SoundAction

Plays the sound Cue (a cue in content/defs/sounds.json) at the player's position, for everyone nearby.

Structs

EffectCueRecord

A sound or flash an effect made (SoundAction, FlashAction), for the presentation to play. It records when and where, and which action of which effect. The effect is its index in EffectRegistry, which is the same on host and clients.

EffectRequest

Something an effect needs done in the world, which the caller of Apply(EffectDefinition, Entity, ref Loadout, in WeaponStats, ref WeaponState, ICollection<EffectRequest>) handles: dropping a pod or a device, or playing a sound or a flash. Index is the action's index in the effect's action list.

Enums

EffectTrigger

When an effect happens (the "trigger" field in effects.json).

PlayerStat

The stats a stat change (ChangeStatAction) can modify. Each is changed by a fraction of its value.
