A boarding action (design doc, "Boarding action: a ship in the fight"): the warship the squad is on is in a fight with the fleet. The fleet's guns hit it on a cadence (each hit counted in HitCount, so everyone aboard feels it); a hit hurts those near it, and sometimes starts a fire or blows a window. Fires spread room to room and burn out, unless the ship's fire suppression is running: its sprinklers put them out, until a hit knocks it out (repaired at its controls in damage control) or its power goes (cutting the reactor kills it). While its batteries fire, the fleet takes damage. The squad silences the batteries and cuts the reactor by holding Interact at each, then takes the bridge; a battery or the reactor lost with its room to a breach is out for good.

Remarks

Keycards are taken by walking over them, by anyone standing (not downed); the squad shares them. A locked door opens when someone standing comes near it with its keycard held. Holding Interact at the terminal starts the hack, which runs while anyone standing is in the terminal's room and pauses while nobody is. Once the hack is done, the mission succeeds when everyone still alive is standing at the extraction point together for a moment. It fails when nobody is standing: everyone down or dead at once (alone, dying is enough).

Reinforcement waves grow with the number of players. A wave's soldiers wait as Pending until a spawn closet can take them: one by the arena or extraction first, nearest the terminal, whose door is shut, with nobody inside and nobody standing within SpawnClearance. They appear inside, out of sight, its door opens, they hunt towards the terminal, and the door shuts once the closet is empty again. A level without closets uses its fallback points, but never one anyone can see or is near.

Runs with the command systems, after the soldiers' AI, so this tick's commands (Interact) are read as given. The mission's state is one MissionState entity; keycards are MissionKey entities, locks MissionLock and closet doors ClosetDoor entities. Spawn(GameWorld, MissionPlan) creates them and Restart(GameWorld) renews them. Doors through bulkheads are SealableDoor entities (gates' beside their locks), shut while Seal(int) has their compartment sealed. A world without them (the range, the outpost) has no mission, and this system does nothing. Allocation-free except on the ticks soldiers spawn.

SendPatrol(int, Vector3, int)

The Director's patrol: up to count soldiers out of the usable spawn closet nearest goal, in a zone the squad has opened, hunting towards it. A source closet (on a service way, by a security office) is favored (SourceFavor), and none is used whose compartment is sealed or that has no way to the goal but through a sealed one. Nothing comes when no closet will do (all in view, near someone, busy, sealed off, or behind a shut gate), a source room (PatrolRooms) nobody is in, near or can see into does instead, the nearest.
