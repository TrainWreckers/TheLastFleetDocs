Whether a part is drawn, collided with, or both.

[Flags]
public enum PartUsage : byte

Fields

Render = 1

Drawn by the renderer.

Collide = 2

Collided with by characters and hit by rays.

Both = Render | Collide
Rays = 4

Hit by rays (shots, grenades, sight, the navigation grid) but has no body for characters to bump into. Used for a face that doesn't need to be a box, such as a ceiling. Rays only hit it from the side it faces.

Body = 8

A body characters bump into, but rays pass through it. Used for the solid behind a Rays face.

Backdrop = 16

Drawn outside what's playable (the sky round a level with windows onto space, its hull's skin, the fleet), so it's left out of its mesh's bounds, which are what's playable.
