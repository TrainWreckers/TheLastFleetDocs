VulkanRenderer

The Vulkan 1.3 renderer. It draws the scene in HDR with multisampling (the static world mesh, flat-shaded with a depth buffer and tinted per part, plus the horde, bodies, world-space effects and the gun in hand), then applies bloom and the tone curve into the swapchain, then draws the 2D overlay and textured UI (Dear ImGui) on top.
