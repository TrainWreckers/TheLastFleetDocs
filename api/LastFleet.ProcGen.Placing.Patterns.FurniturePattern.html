A furniture pattern (a furnishing's patterns in rules.json): a small grid of half-meter cells, drawn as the room is seen from above, saying what has to be where (a wall behind, open floor in front, a desk beside) and what it places there. A room finds every spot and turn it fits, scores them, and keeps the best until it has as many as its count (LastFleet.ProcGen.Placing.Patterns.PatternPlacer).
