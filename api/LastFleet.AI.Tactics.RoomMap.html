A generated level's rooms and the doorways between them, so soldiers can fight indoors. Answers which room a point is in, which doorways lead out of a room, and the route from room to room.

Remarks

Built from the level layout, which places its rooms on a grid of cells and stories. Hand-built levels don't have one, and soldiers fight there the old way.

Route(int, int, Span<int>, int, Func<int, bool>?)

Finds the route from room from to room to that crosses the fewest doorways, and writes those doorways into route in order. The route never passes through avoidRoom (unless it's the destination) or through doorways isClosed reports as locked. Doesn't allocate.
