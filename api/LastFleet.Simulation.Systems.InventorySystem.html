Remarks

A player can drop a stack on the floor in front of them, move a stack between slots, use a consumable from a quick slot, or put down the weapon in hand. Using a consumable runs its effect: a medkit's health, an ammo pack's rounds, setting down a pod beacon's cargo pod or a breaching charge in front, or a sound or flash in EffectCueLog. The weapon is lowered while the item is in use, and the item is spent when it's done.

Each op is applied once, tracked by its serial. Nothing happens while the player is downed or dead. Fitting weapon parts needs the loot data, so LastFleet.Loot's part fit system handles that, before this system runs.

Runs after the weapon pickup system and before the weapon system, so a weapon lowered to use an item is lowered on the same tick. Only the host runs it; a client sees the result of an op in its next snapshot. Doesn't allocate, apart from the entities a drop creates.
