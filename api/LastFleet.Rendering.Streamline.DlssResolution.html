Jitter(long, int)

The camera jitter of a frame, in pixels from the pixel's center (x right, y down; each from -0.5 to 0.5): the Halton sequence in bases 2 and 3, from its second point (its first is the center), cycling every phases frames. This is what Streamline's jitterOffset is given.

JitterPhases(VkExtent2D, VkExtent2D)

How many different sub-pixel positions the camera jitter cycles through: eight times the ratio of the display's pixels to the rendered ones (DLSS programming guide, 3.7.1.1), and never fewer than 32 (DLSS-RR integration guide, 3.6), as RTXPT does it.
