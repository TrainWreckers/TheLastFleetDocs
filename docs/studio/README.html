Studio guides

Step-by-step tutorials for LastFleet Studio. Where the reference covers every field and every button, these walk you through real tasks, one step at a time, with a screenshot of what you should see along the way.

Every screenshot here is Studio itself, doing the step it shows. So if your screen doesn't match... either Studio has changed since the guide was written, or something's gone wrong! Either way, it's worth a look.

Studio

New to Studio? Start with Getting started: opening it, finding your way around, editing a field, undo, saving, and seeing your change in the game.

Then pick the mode for what you want to change:

Guide Use it to...
Loot Tune what drops: rarities, loot tables, relics, and the odds in the Loot Lab.
Armory Tune the guns: frames, makers, parts, traits and impact. Check one in 3D or try it on the range.
Items Tune everything else you carry: medkits and other effects, supplies, the refinery and the bench.
Flow See how materials, recipes, items and loot connect, and find broken names.
Missions Walk through generated levels, ask a room why it's there, run checks over many seeds, and tune breaches, doors and the campaign.
Levels Change what levels are built from: tags, styles, archetypes, rules, props, networks, loot, typing, plans and districts.
Bodies Check animation on the turntable, edit the graph and the rig, tune reloads and fit hit shapes.
Behavior Make enemies fight differently: the sandbox, cover, the behavior tree, intent scoring, difficulty and variants.
Look Tune surface materials under each light, and the skies.
Models Pick what each prop model is collided with, and fix what Studio flags.

A couple of tips that hold for every mode:

  • Nothing is written until you save. Edit freely: the status bar at the bottom tells you what's not saved, and Ctrl+Z takes it back.
  • Studio can open straight into a step. Most guides show the command line for where they start (--mode, --select, --tab and friends). Run dotnet run --project src/LastFleet.Studio -- --help for the full list.
